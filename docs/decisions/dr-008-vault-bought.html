DR-008Tokenisation vault bought, not built

Real values live in a bought tokenisation vault of the Skyflow, VGS and Basis Theory class, hosted in each market's region; humans, the AI and almost all of our software handle aliases. We buy the layer because holding secrets and being audited for it is a product, and because the provider's regional hosting is part of how the residency map is honoured. Which vendor is an open item.

1Decision

We buy the tokenisation vault. Every sensitive value, an identity number, a card, an account, a date of birth, a passport, enters a bought vault of the Skyflow, VGS and Basis Theory class and leaves it in only two ways: through the substitution component, into a surface, or as the address of an outbound message, resolved inside the vault's own edge proxy and handed to the channel provider, never to us. Humans, the AI model and almost all of our software handle aliases: {{pan}}, {{card.primary}}, {{nhs-number}}. The vault is hosted regionally per market. It is one of the four bought components in the build-versus-buy table in 06. Its edge is a tokenising proxy the provider runs in the market's region under our detector policy: the channel webhook, the device form and every rail response terminate there, so the intake gateway and the passport adapters receive aliases and never hold a raw byte. Its SDK is present in exactly two places of ours, the substitutor and the hash service; the gateway, the adapters, the model runtime, the worker console and the logging pipeline hold none and have no network route to the vault, which is the gate 06 checks at stage 1.

1.1Why the layer is bought

Three reasons, in order of weight. The vault's job is to be dull and audited: key custody, hardware-backed storage, rotation, attestation, breach response. That is a product others sell and are examined on; every hour spent rebuilding it is an hour not spent on the substitutor, which nobody sells. Second, regional hosting. The brief's ledger fixes that Bengaluru staff processing UK or EU personal data is a restricted cross-border transfer needing contractual safeguards, and names the provider's regional hosting as part of why this layer is bought. India is moving the same way: the DPDP Rules 2025 bring cross-border transfer obligations into force in May 2027 verified [1]. A vault instance in each market's region lets the residency map in 03 say one plain thing: sensitive values stay in region; only aliases travel to the floor. Third, in India a vault is already a regulated construct: UIDAI's Circular 8 of 2025 updates its Aadhaar Data Vault and hardware security module requirements verified [2], and a provider's fitness against such rules, market by market, is one of the tests in 1.3 below, carried by the vendor item in 07.

1.2What buying does not delegate

The alias grammar is ours. The substitutor is ours, and it stays ours even where a provider offers an injection or detokenising proxy of its own, because the one component with detokenise rights must be small enough to audit line by line and specific to our three surfaces; the tokenising edge proxy is the one provider feature this design leans on, and it carries two roles and no more: it tokenises what enters, and its channel connector, an address-only role beside the substitutor's value role and part of the proxy contract, resolves the address alias of an outbound message, {{phone}} or a WhatsApp or iMessage handle, only to hand the message to the channel provider, returning nothing to any process of ours and placing nothing into any surface; a value is detokenised into a surface by the substitutor alone. The detector policy that proxy runs under is ours, and so is the hash service, which turns the vault's keyed digests into match-or-no-match answers for the maskers and the verifier and holds no detokenise right. The policy-layer and envelope checks before every injection are ours. The vault sees the bytes of a message, an attachment or a rail response at its edge proxy for as long as tokenising takes, the audio of a voice note while the proxy transcribes it, a value in and an alias out, and a detokenise call from one client identity; it never sees a task, a step, a surface or a person. In the threat model the provider is a named path, not a trusted box, and the path has two halves: the account, since whoever administers it could otherwise reveal a value, and the proxy, since whoever runs it could otherwise read a whole message. Four controls close it: no role on the provider account can reveal or detokenise, and the dashboard reveal is disabled or absent by contract; the substitutor's client credential is the only detokenise principal and lives in a secret store no person reads, the channel connector's resolution of an address at the proxy being address-only, a term of the proxy contract rather than a detokenise right, and returning nothing to us; the proxy holds the bytes of one message, attachment or rail response in memory for as long as tokenising takes and no longer, transcribes a voice note inside that same boundary, runs our detector policy, and keeps its request and error logs value-free by contract, bodies never logged and no debugging view of one, so that the provider's staff access controls, attestations and regional hosting are the bought layer's guarantee for what the proxy holds, a legal item the vendor evaluation in 07 tests and this record does not assume; and the provider's access logs are exported and reconciled against the access ledger, so no detokenisation happens unrecorded.

1.3No vendor is chosen

The class is fixed; the vendor is not. The research sprint for this edition did not reach the vault vendor class, so nothing on this page asserts what any provider offers, and the choice is an open item in 07, owned by the founding engineer and triggered before the substitutor is built. The tests a candidate must pass are ours: hold Indian residents' values in an Indian region that meets UIDAI's Aadhaar Data Vault and HSM requirements for any Aadhaar number it holds, and UK residents' values in a UK or EU region; run its tokenising edge proxy in each region with our detectors loaded, terminating the channel webhook, the device form and every rail response, transcribing a voice note inside that boundary, and carrying the channel connector that delivers our outbound messages, resolving the address alias only to hand a message to the channel provider and returning nothing to us, with bodies never logged at the proxy and its staff access controls and attestations covering the proxy as they cover the vault; expose detokenisation to one client identity only; issue keyed digests of each value in its normalised forms to our hash service without releasing the value, so the maskers and the verifier can match an echo; attest to a standard a legal pack can cite; and survive the re-tokenisation swap test that 06 requires before the pilot opens. If no provider of the named class passes the regional test, the buy stands and the class widens to a regional provider meeting the same tests; building the vault is not the fallback without reopening this record.

2Rejected alternative(s) and their cost

Build the vault ourselves
We become custodians of secrets in three regions before we have a pilot, and prove key custody to auditors in each. The engineers who should be proving the substitutor would be proving a storage product, and the residency map would rest on hosting we do not run.
Encrypt fields in place in our own database
The real value stays in our data path. Every component holding the key can read it, so the alias rule becomes a convention rather than a boundary, and backups, replicas and logs carry ciphertext our own staff can decrypt.
Rely on the rails' own tokens
Card-network tokens, where a market offers them, cover cards and nothing else: not an identity number, a date of birth or a health number. They are per country and do not reach our surfaces; a token cannot be spoken on a phone line. The rails are adapters, and the vault sits behind all of them.
Choose the vendor now
A choice made without the research would be a choice from memory, which the brief forbids for anything external. Deferring costs nothing: the substitutor's interface to the vault is fixed here, and the vendor sits behind it.

3What this closes off

  • A real value at rest in any store we run: database, cache, queue, backup or log.
  • The vault SDK anywhere but the substitutor and the hash service; the build checks its absence from every other artefact, the intake gateway and the passport adapters included, and network policy denies them the vault's endpoints.
  • A raw byte of a message, an upload, a voice note or a rail response held by a process of ours: each terminates at the vault's edge proxy and reaches us tokenised.
  • A vault region that disagrees with a market's residency map, or one region serving three markets.
  • A provider-supplied reveal screen, detokenise endpoint or detokenising proxy reachable from the worker console, the model runtime, the logging pipeline or a person's provider login: no role on the provider account can reveal or detokenise, the substitutor's credential is the only detokenise principal, and the provider's access logs are reconciled against the access ledger.
  • A detokenisation for a task whose envelope the user's own device has not signed, or whose envelope has expired or been withdrawn: the substitutor checks the device-signed confirmation the step context carries, so the vault's credential and the user's key are two locks, and neither opens the other.
  • A message body, an attachment, a voice note or a transcript persisting at the proxy, in a provider log, an error trace or a debugging view: the proxy holds one payload's bytes in memory for as long as tokenising takes, and its logs are value-free by contract, a term the vendor evaluation tests.
  • A channel connector that returns a resolved address, or anything else, to a process of ours, or that can place a value into a surface: its role is address-only and ends at the channel provider.
  • Delegating the substitutor to the provider, whatever it offers.
  • A provider we cannot leave: the re-tokenisation swap test runs before the pilot, not after the first outage.

AI.fred is a personal AI that builds your context, task by task, until it can act in your personal life the way someone who has known you for years would. It handles the ordinary things, a table, a return, a rental search, a trip, and it is built so that it can also be trusted with the ones you dread: the bank, the visa, the claim, the admission. The dreaded tasks are why people come. The ordinary ones are why they stay, and why the context compounds fast enough to be worth anything. AI.fred is a working title.
