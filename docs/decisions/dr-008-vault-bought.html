DR-008Tokenisation vault bought, not built

Real values live in a bought tokenisation vault of the Skyflow, VGS and Basis Theory class, hosted in each market's region; humans, the AI and almost all of our software handle aliases. We buy the layer because holding secrets and being audited for it is a product, and because the provider's regional hosting is part of how the residency map is honoured. Which vendor is an open item.

On this page

1Decision

We buy the tokenisation vault. Every sensitive value, an identity number, a card, an account, a date of birth, a passport, enters a bought vault of the Skyflow, VGS and Basis Theory class and leaves it only through the substitution component. Humans, the AI model and almost all of our software handle aliases: {{pan}}, {{card.primary}}, {{nhs-number}}. The vault is hosted regionally per market. It is one of the four bought components in the build-versus-buy table in 06, and its SDK is present in exactly three places: the substitutor and the two tokenising edges, the intake gateway and the adapter boundary.

1.1Why the layer is bought

Three reasons, in order of weight. The vault's job is to be dull and audited: key custody, hardware-backed storage, rotation, attestation, breach response. That is a product others sell and are examined on; every hour spent rebuilding it is an hour not spent on the substitutor, which nobody sells. Second, regional hosting. The brief's ledger fixes that Bengaluru staff processing UK or EU personal data is a restricted cross-border transfer needing contractual safeguards, and names the provider's regional hosting as part of why this layer is bought. India is moving the same way: the DPDP Rules 2025 bring cross-border transfer obligations into force in May 2027 verified [1]. A vault instance in each market's region lets the residency map in 03 say one plain thing: sensitive values stay in region; only aliases travel to the floor. Third, in India a vault is already a regulated construct: UIDAI's Circular 8 of 2025 updates its Aadhaar Data Vault and hardware security module requirements verified [2], and a provider's fitness against such rules, market by market, is one of the tests 07 sets.

1.2What buying does not delegate

The alias grammar is ours. The substitutor is ours, and it stays ours even where a provider offers an injection or proxy feature of its own, because the one component with detokenise rights must be small enough to audit line by line and specific to our three surfaces. The two tokenising edges are ours. The policy-layer and envelope checks before every injection are ours. The vault sees a value in and an alias out, and a detokenise call from one client identity; it never sees a task, a step, a surface or a person. In the threat model the provider is a named path, not a trusted box.

1.3No vendor is chosen

The class is fixed; the vendor is not. The research sprint for this edition did not reach the vault vendor class, so nothing on this page asserts what any provider offers, and the choice is an open item in 07, owned by the founding engineer and triggered before the substitutor is built. The tests a candidate must pass are ours: hold Indian residents' values in an Indian region and UK residents' values in a UK or EU region; expose detokenisation to one client identity only; support known-value matching for the echo-masking edge without releasing the value; attest to a standard a legal pack can cite; and survive the re-tokenisation swap test that 06 requires before the pilot opens.

2Rejected alternative(s) and their cost

Build the vault ourselves
We become custodians of secrets in three regions before we have a pilot, and prove key custody to auditors in each. The engineers who should be proving the substitutor would be proving a storage product, and the residency map would rest on hosting we do not run.
Encrypt fields in place in our own database
The real value stays in our data path. Every component holding the key can read it, so the alias rule becomes a convention rather than a boundary, and backups, replicas and logs carry ciphertext our own staff can decrypt.
Rely on the rails' own tokens
Card-network tokens, where a market offers them, cover cards and nothing else: not an identity number, a date of birth or a health number. They are per country and do not reach our surfaces; a token cannot be spoken on a phone line. The rails are adapters, and the vault sits behind all of them.
Choose the vendor now
A choice made without the research would be a choice from memory, which the brief forbids for anything external. Deferring costs nothing: the substitutor's interface to the vault is fixed here, and the vendor sits behind it.

3What this closes off

  • A real value at rest in any store we run: database, cache, queue, backup or log.
  • The vault SDK anywhere but the substitutor, the intake gateway's edge and the adapter boundary; the build checks its absence from every other artefact.
  • A vault region that disagrees with a market's residency map, or one region serving three markets.
  • A provider-supplied reveal screen, detokenise endpoint or proxy reachable from the worker console, the model runtime or the logging pipeline.
  • Delegating the substitutor to the provider, whatever it offers.
  • A provider we cannot leave: the re-tokenisation swap test runs before the pilot, not after the first outage.

4Status

Fixed decision, taken by the founder and recorded in the brief's ledger on 2026-09-02. The alias model and the substitutor are specified in 03, the buy is sequenced as stage 1 in 06, and the vendor choice is open in 07 with its owner and trigger.
