1Decision

Withdrawn on 2026-09-13. The deferral below was the founder's position of 2026-09-02 and is no longer the record's: 05 now carries a modelled economics, the four levers that move it and a round plan, and the measurement protocol is the instrument that replaces the priors. This record stands as the position that was replaced, and section 1 reads as it did.

Task economics, pricing and unit costs are deferred. The founder has ruled them a post-Series A or B problem, and the ruling is fixed in the brief's ledger. The business section is a deliberate one-page stub with three parts and nothing else: the deferral, the trigger for reopening, and what will be needed when it reopens.

1.1Why the thesis stands without it

The argument in 00 is that the wedge is high-consequence tasks and that the flat-fee players cannot follow into them, because what they lack is a trust architecture, not a price point. That argument is true at any price the pilot later reveals, so the thesis is written with no economics in it, as the ledger requires, and a sceptical reader is asked to judge the moat rather than a margin. When an investor asks what a task costs, the honest answer in this edition is that the harness is built to measure it: minutes per step class, per vertical, per market, from the step trace, and pool utilisation from the shift wheel, once a pilot has run. That measurement is a design property, and it is the only economic choice this publication makes.

1.2The trigger

The question reopens when a Series A or Series B closes, whichever the founder names as the point at which the venture must price and cost itself against its pilot's verdict data. The trigger is the founder's to fire, and it was stated with its owner in 05; 07 does not repeat it, because a dated ruling is not an open item. Until it fires, no economics section is written and the stub stays one page.

2Rejected alternative(s) and their cost

Model the economics now
Every input would be a guess: minutes per task before a task has run, pool sizes before a shift has been staffed, price points before a customer has given a verdict. The brief forbids invented numbers, and the founder's bar requires the thesis to carry itself with no economics; a model built from guesses would fail both and, worse, would be believed.
Use the flat-fee players' prices as a proxy for ours
The landscape in 00 records their pricing models as chipped claims because a reader needs to know what the field charges. As a proxy for us they measure the wrong thing: errands priced flat, not consequence-bearing tasks priced against a trust guarantee. Carrying them over would put pricing on the thesis page, where none is permitted.
A placeholder model labelled illustrative
Illustrative numbers acquire authority by repetition. An investor reads the table and not the label, and the first time a figure is quoted back the label is gone. The only safe illustrative number is one that carries no pricing meaning, which the ledger already allows.
Omit the business section altogether
A gap reads as an oversight, and the brief forbids silence about anything left undone. The stub says what is deferred, by whom, until when, and what will be needed, so nobody has to guess whether economics was forgotten.

3What this closes off

  • A price point, a cost per task, a margin, a headcount model or a market-size claim of our own on any page; the stub itself states the deferral and carries none.
  • Cost figures attached to the shift wheel, the tiers or the site controls in 04, and vendor prices in the build-versus-buy table in 06.
  • An unlabelled number of our own that could be read as economics, and an illustrative number that carries pricing meaning.
  • Reopening the question by anyone but the founder, or before the trigger fires.
  • A thesis that needs a number to carry its argument.

4Status

Withdrawn by the founder on 2026-09-13: 05 carries a modelled economics instead of the stub, the harness now checks for that, and 07 no longer holds it as an item, recording only the arithmetic that measurement replaces. The step trace and the verdict record that will feed the model when it reopens are specified in 02 and DR-002. Confirmed on 2026-09-10: the endgame DR-019 records states no economics, and the data story it names is deferred with them, an open item in 07 that reopens with 05.

AI.fred is a personal AI that builds your context, task by task, until it can act in your personal life the way someone who has known you for years would. It handles the ordinary things, a table, a return, a rental search, a trip, and it is built so that it can also be trusted with the ones you dread: the bank, the visa, the claim, the admission. The dreaded tasks are why people come. The ordinary ones are why they stay, and why the context compounds fast enough to be worth anything. AI.fred is a working title. This publication is a design record, not an offer.
