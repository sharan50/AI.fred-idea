DRDecisions

Twenty-seven records hold every decision that shapes the venture and this publication. Twenty-two are fixed, one of them withdrawn and kept as the position it replaced. Three are amber with their triggers: the front door, the staging of the floor and the geography sequence. Nothing here is reopened; an objection goes to 07 and the build complies.

2How a record works

A record has four parts, in a fixed order, and a fifth when it is amber: the decision, stated in one paragraph and then in its substance; the rejected alternative or alternatives and what each would have cost; what the decision closes off, as a list a reader can hold the publication to; its status, with the date and who took it; and, for an amber record, the trigger that resolves it. A record with no rejected alternative is not a decision but a description, and the harness rejects it.

A fixed decision is not reopened in this publication. When the build formed an objection to one, the objection was written down in 07 with how the build complied, and the decision stood. That is deliberate: a design that reopens its foundations page by page is not a design, and an investor or an engineer reading one record should be able to trust that the others agree with it.

To add a record, copy the newest one, take the next number, keep its four headings and the stamp, add the fifth if the record is amber, link it from this ledger and list it in the manifest. The harness checks all of that. A record that supersedes another says so in its status and carries the stamp Superseded decision on the record it replaces.

AI.fred is a personal AI that builds your context, task by task, until it can act in your personal life the way someone who has known you for years would. It handles the ordinary things, a table, a return, a rental search, a trip, and it is built so that it can also be trusted with the ones you dread: the bank, the visa, the claim, the admission. The dreaded tasks are why people come. The ordinary ones are why they stay, and why the context compounds fast enough to be worth anything. AI.fred is a working title. This publication is a design record, not an offer.
