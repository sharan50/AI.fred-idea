On this page

1Decision

The United Kingdom opens between months 8 and 14 rather than as a later market, with acquisition focus genuinely moved there rather than treated as secondary. amber India is the delivery base, the workflow laboratory and a secondary revenue market.

Two gates govern any market: 400 users in the United Kingdom and 600 in the United States to carry the local cost base. assumption No market is opened that cannot plausibly reach 500 users within twelve months of entry, and no second market is opened until the first has cleared its gate. The playbook that a market runs through is 06, 4; the arithmetic is 05, 8 and the model page behind it.

2Rejected alternative(s) and their cost

The published sequence: the UK around month 20, the US around month 36, both secondary
Modelling says that version is worse than not expanding at all, because the standing cost of a foreign entity arrives long before the users do: it nearly triples the conservative cash requirement while lowering the probability of profitability. assumption
India only, no foreign entity
Coherent and cheaper, and it gives up the purchasing-power arbitrage that a US user at $105 and an Indian user at $52 cost the same to serve. assumption It remains the fallback if the gate is not met.
Opening both foreign markets at once
Rejected by the second gate: two standing cost bases before either has users.

3What this closes off

  • It commits capital to foreign acquisition at a cost per user roughly ten times the Indian figure, against a price roughly twice as high. assumption That works on lifetime value and is expensive on cash.
  • A market opened on optimism rather than on its gate, and a second market opened before the first has cleared.
  • The description of India as the first of three equal markets. It is the delivery base and the laboratory, and its revenue is secondary.
  • A foreign entity stood up before the gate that pays for it, with the counsel, the registration and the standing cost that follow.

4Status

Amber decision, taken by the founder on 2026-09-13, with the trigger in section 4. DR-006 is staged against the same event, because the first foreign market live is one of the two things that calls the attested site into being.

5Trigger

A priced United Kingdom waiting list aimed at non-resident households, measured for conversion. It is amber because the case rests on an untested assumption, that we can acquire abroad from a standing start with no brand and no local team, and because a well-funded prior attempt at a comparable service in the United States closed in 2025 after reaching a few thousand households. The waiting list is an open item with an owner in 07; until it answers, the dates in 06, 4.1 are a plan and not a commitment.

AI.fred is a personal AI that builds your context, task by task, until it can act in your personal life the way someone who has known you for years would. It handles the ordinary things, a table, a return, a rental search, a trip, and it is built so that it can also be trusted with the ones you dread: the bank, the visa, the claim, the admission. The dreaded tasks are why people come. The ordinary ones are why they stay, and why the context compounds fast enough to be worth anything. AI.fred is a working title. This publication is a design record, not an offer.
