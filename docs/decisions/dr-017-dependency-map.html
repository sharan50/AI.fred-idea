DR-017A dependency map of the record, kept as a tool

Before this publication is changed on purpose, its dependencies are mapped: one graph of typed edges and faceted nodes at the grain of decisions, mechanisms, invariants and closed lists, kept under the repository's tools directory and never published as a page. A node carries a name and the places where the pages state the thing, never a paraphrase of it, and the harness checks every pointer on every pass. A proposed change is run through the map first, so that what must change with it is known before an editor touches a page.

On this page

1Decision

The dependencies inside this publication are mapped, and the map is a tool. It lives under tools/depmap/ beside the verification harness, is never served, and has no build step, no framework and no dependency, as DR-010 requires of anything in the publishing path. It is one graph: nodes for the two fatal failures, the thesis premises, the brief's ledger items, every decision record and every alternative it rejected, the components of the harness, the invariants a record closes off, the closed lists the pages restate, the rows of the policy flag table, the roadmap stages, the open items and objections of 07, and the residual findings of the seventh adversarial pass; edges of six kinds, each with a stated direction; facets for layer, fixity, fatal failure, surface and market. A node carries a name of at most eighty characters and a list of loci, each a page path and an anchor the harness already resolves, so the HTML stays the single canonical source and the map can never become a second statement of the design. The curated graph and a reference layer regenerated from the pages are both committed, and tools/verify.mjs runs the map's check on every pass: a locus that no longer resolves, a closed list restated in words that have drifted, or a stale reference layer fails the build in the same file:line message form as every other failure. The map's first use is to compute, for a proposed change, everything upstream that justifies the thing being changed, everything downstream that specifies, restates or depends on it, and everything sideways that it would contradict, before any page is edited; several proposed changes are then planned as one batch from the union of their closures. A proposed change is not a node in this edition: the map records the publication as it is.

1.1The grain

The unit is the decision, the mechanism, the invariant or the closed list, never the page. At page grain the answer to "what does this touch" is every page, because the publication is dense and self-referential: 03 alone carries more than a hundred and fifty internal links. Its closed lists are restated far more often than the harness had checked: the six committing actions on seven pages, the seven identity acts on five, the nine outcome codes on four, the point-of-no-return question on two, and until this record the harness compared exactly one restatement, the state-machine invariant that DR-005 fixes in 02 and the worker console. The map names every such restatement as a locus on the list's node, holds the expected members once, and checks each locus against them, in the words the page uses, in the code form the tables use, or as a deliberate partial restatement that omits named members. Where the record already disagrees with itself, the locus carries a waiver naming the residual finding or the open item that records the drift, and the waiver fails the moment the drift is fixed, so it cannot linger.

1.2What a node may carry

A name, a kind, its facets, its loci with their roles (canonical, restates, partial, mentions, home, evidence), and for a closed list its members and the mode its restatements are checked in. Nothing else about the design: no summary, no rationale, no restatement of a mechanism in the map's own words. A locus points at an anchor and may add a phrase the anchored section must contain, or a table row by its first cell, so that a locus for a flag-table row or a roadmap stage is as precise as one for a heading. The reference layer records every anchor, table and internal link under the docs directory, so a renamed id fails twice, once in the harness's own link check and once in the map's.

1.3Where it is specified

The closed vocabularies of kinds, roles, facets and edge kinds, the locus grammar, the direction of each edge kind, the check modes and the change protocol are in tools/depmap/SCHEMA.md. The commands are extract, check, impact, reopens, batch, at, show, list, selftest and view in tools/depmap.mjs; the harness calls check as its eighth section. Each batch of proposed changes is recorded under tools/depmap/batches/ with the seeds that produced it and the closure the tool returned. The state machine of 02 is the precedent: a rule that nothing checks can drift by a word, and the map extends that precedent to every closed list the record restates.

2Rejected alternative(s) and their cost

A page-level map
Pages and the links between them are already recorded, and they answer the wrong question. The unit of change is a mechanism stated on five pages, not a page; a page graph reports everything or nothing, and gives the restatement check no grain to work at.
Several maps, one per dimension
A map of decisions, a map of components, a map of legal items. The same mechanism appears in each, and the seams between them are where seven adversarial passes found the residual findings. One graph with typed edges and faceted nodes is queried along any dimension without a second copy of anything.
A wiki, a graph database or a diagram tool
A second representation of the design that drifts from the first, which is the reason DR-010 rejected a Markdown mirror; a dependency the publishing path must not carry; and a store the harness cannot check without running a service. A JSON file the harness reads costs none of that.
A published, interactive map page
DR-010 bans inline scripts and frameworks, so a browsable graph could not be built inside the publication's own rules, and a map that is pleasant to browse invites reading the map instead of the record. A static drawing of the map may earn a place in a later edition; the instrument itself stays a tool. Reversed in part on 2026-09-10 by the founder, who asked for the map to be reachable from the published workspace: the three-dimensional view is served at docs/depmap/ under an exception DR-010 now names, at the cost stated here, and the instrument is still the JSON the harness checks; the page is generated from it and never edited.
The internal links as the map
Eleven hundred references cannot tell a canonical statement from a mention, a restatement from a citation, or a rejected alternative from a decision. They are kept as the mechanical layer the map's queries consult for inbound links, and are never mistaken for the map.

3What this closes off

  • A map, dependency list or impact table of this publication kept anywhere but tools/depmap/, or in any form the harness does not check.
  • A node label that restates the design in the map's words; a node carries a name and pointers, never a paraphrase.
  • A locus written any way but a page path and an anchor the harness resolves, with a phrase or a table row where an anchor is not precise enough.
  • A closed list checked by eye where the map names it, and a restatement added to a page without its locus added to the list's node.
  • A proposed change modelled as a node in this edition; a change is a set of seeds run through the map, and the map records the record as it stands.
  • A build step or a dependency for the map; it runs on the same Node the harness runs on and nothing else. The one served artefact is the view at docs/depmap/, generated by the same tool, committed, and failed by the harness when stale, when indexed or when it loads anything from outside; it is a view of the map and never the map.
  • Editing a fixed decision, a mechanism stated on more than one page, or a closed list without first running the change through the map and recording the batch.

4Status

Fixed decision, taken by the founder on 2026-09-10. It postdates the brief's ledger, which fixed the publication's other structural choices, and it reopens none of them. The map is specified in tools/depmap/SCHEMA.md, populated in tools/depmap/graph.json, and checked by tools/verify.mjs on every pass. The residual findings the seventh adversarial pass left, carried as an open item in 07, are the map's first curated conflicts, and the drift the map found while it was built is recorded in its first batch under tools/depmap/batches/ rather than fixed on the branch that built the map. Revised 2026-09-13: the founder asked for the map to be active from the published workspace, so view --site writes the three-dimensional view to docs/depmap/, listed in the manifest, linked from the index, kept fresh by the harness, and served with the rest of the site; DR-010 records the one exception this makes to its closure.

AI.fred is a personal AI that builds your context, task by task, until it can act in your personal life the way someone who has known you for years would. It handles the ordinary things, a table, a return, a rental search, a trip, and it is built so that it can also be trusted with the ones you dread: the bank, the visa, the claim, the admission. The dreaded tasks are why people come. The ordinary ones are why they stay, and why the context compounds fast enough to be worth anything. AI.fred is a working title. This publication is a design record, not an offer.
