DR-007A universal core with per-country adapters, legal pack and shift

One harness, built once and country-agnostic, carries every trust property. Each market adds passport adapters to its rails, a legal pack and a data-residency map, plus a shift. Expansion is that playbook repeated, and it never touches the core.

On this page

1Decision

We build one universal core and add a thin, named layer per country. The core is country-agnostic, and 02 lists its eleven components: the planner, the classifier, task cards, the alias vault and the substitution component, authority envelopes, the state machine, verification, the worker console, the access ledger, the intake gateway and the policy layer's mechanism. Per country we add passport adapters to its identity and financial rails, a legal pack and a data-residency map; operationally, a shift. India is the build base, the US and the UK are the commercial ground, and we build for all three from the first line.

1.1What lives in the core

Everything that makes the two fatal failures structurally impossible, so that each is proven once. The alias type system is universal: {{pan}}, {{ni-number}} and {{ssn}} are three values of one type, so the planner and the substitutor treat an identity number the same way in three countries. The policy layer's mechanism is core; its flag table's content is per country and travels with the legal pack. The rule of classification, the state-machine invariant and the outcome-code vocabulary have one text each, which no country edits.

1.2What lives in the adapter

A passport adapter is the per-country integration to a rail; the device bounce-back is not an adapter but a universal component every market shares. The rails are the brief's fixed list, drawn in 02 and researched in 03; every rail response terminates at the vault's edge proxy deployed in front of the adapter, which tokenises it against a declared schema in which sensitivity is stated per field, so the adapter receives aliases and non-sensitive attributes and never holds a raw byte. The adapter reaches the rail only through that proxy, holds no vault SDK and has no route to the vault, and the rail token it presents lives in the vault and is injected into the call by the proxy; the planner sees aliases whichever rail produced them. The region key has two parts: the user's market fixes the vault instance, the residency safeguards and the region the substitutor runs in; the institution's market fixes only the rail. So a UK resident acting on an Indian institution keeps their values and their substitutor in the UK region and reaches the Indian rail through the India adapter across the same boundary as any placement; the residency map in 03 is to carry that rule.

The rails show why the adapter is a country's, not the core's. India's DigiLocker releases a document only on the user's own OAuth authorisation verified [1], and the RBI's Account Aggregator direction, in its 2017 text, reserves the information-user role to regulated entities and bars an aggregator from transactions verified [2]; whether that text is still in force is a 07 legal item. So how a rail is reached, directly, through a regulated partner or by a bounce to the user's device, is a per-country answer, held as a flag with a legal item behind it, per DR-003.

1.3The legal pack and the residency map

The legal pack carries the four things the core reads at runtime, as 02 defines them: the filled flag table, the data-residency map, the market's refusal list, which the planner consults before any step, and the market's entries in the step-type catalogue, whose schema is core and whose values per step type the classifier, the surfaces and the delay buffers read. Behind those sit the jurisdiction's KYC last-mile items to verify and the contractual safeguards the market needs. One safeguard is fixed in the ledger: Bengaluru staff processing UK or EU data is a restricted cross-border transfer needing contractual safeguards, part of why the vault is bought, per DR-008. The residency map, named beside the pack in the ledger, is at runtime the pack's second item: where each class of data sits per market, sensitive values in the vault's regional instance and the rest with us; 03 draws it.

1.4The playbook

A new country is an adapter, a legal pack and a shift, in that order, as 06 sequences it. If a country needs a change to the core, the core was not universal: that is a defect to fix for every market, not a customisation for one.

2Rejected alternative(s) and their cost

Build for India first, fork for the west later
The fork carries rupee, Aadhaar and UPI assumptions into the planner and the envelope, then multiplies: three harnesses, each re-proving the two fatals, the invariant and the rule of classification drifting between them.
Build for one market only
The ledger places commercial viability in the US and the UK; a one-market build defers the markets that carry the business, and the later port is the fork above.
Adapters that hand real values to the planner
New entry points for real values into the model runtime: the alias model would hold at the gateway and fail at every rail, and the threat model would gain a path per adapter per country.
One global deployment with no residency map
The cross-border transfer question goes unanswered: Bengaluru staff processing UK data with no contractual safeguard, the legal pack's to-verify items unanswered, the vault's regional hosting unused.

3What this closes off

  • A component in the core that knows which country it is in; country is data in the adapter, the legal pack and the residency map.
  • A rail integration that talks to the planner, a worker or the context store in real values; an adapter that holds a raw rail response, the vault SDK or a route to the vault; or an adapter with detokenise rights.
  • An expansion that requires a change to the core.
  • Sensitive values hosted outside the market's region, or workers processing a market's data before its legal pack is in place.
  • An adapter that bypasses the device bounce-back for an act the flag table says bounces.
  • A second alias type system, or a second text for any rule the core owns.

4Status

Fixed decision, taken by the founder and recorded in the brief's ledger on 2026-09-02. 02 draws the seam between core and adapter, 03 specifies the rails and the residency map, and 06 sequences the expansion playbook.

AI.fred is a personal AI that builds your context, task by task, until it can act in your personal life the way someone who has known you for years would. It handles the ordinary things, a table, a return, a rental search, a trip, and it is built so that it can also be trusted with the ones you dread: the bank, the visa, the claim, the admission. The dreaded tasks are why people come. The ordinary ones are why they stay, and why the context compounds fast enough to be worth anything. AI.fred is a working title. This publication is a design record, not an offer.
