DR-027No name ever reaches a worker

A worker never sees or states a name, the user’s or any other person’s, on any surface and in any channel. Context is what lets a generalist do the difficult work; context plus a name is an identification, and context alone is not. So the name is the thing that never travels, and a task whose party process cannot proceed without one is outside our capability rather than a task with a careful workaround.

On this page

1Decision

No name reaches a worker. Not the user's, not a dependant's, not a third party's who is a natural person: not on a card, not in a caption, not in a script line, not in a result field, and not out of a worker's own mouth on a call. There is no first-name exception, no initials, and no "as it appears on the card". The rule is absolute because the thing it protects is not the name.

1.1Why it has to be absolute

A card carries context, and it has to. The complex, moat-defining work is only possible because a worker can see what the task is about: a specialist referral, a visa refusal, a disputed charge, a tenancy. That context is what makes the job doable and it is also, on its own, close to useless for finding the person: a generalist holding a set of circumstances and no name cannot identify anybody. Add a name and the same context becomes an identification, reliably, in a search box, in minutes. The exposure is not the name and it is not the context. It is the pair. Since the context cannot be taken away without taking away the work, the name is what must never be present.

1.2What this excludes on a call

A call is eligible only where the account has already been mapped by the automated identity submission at the start of it, which DR-026 fixes as a property of the party rather than a judgement on the line. The worker joins a call that is already attached to the right record. If, after that, the party's process needs the worker to state a name, or would tell the worker something that identifies the user, the task is excluded from our capability. It does not bounce, it is not attempted with a careful script, and it is not mitigated: a channel that can be made safe only by a person remembering a rule is not fool-proof, and this is the one failure we do not accept being probabilistic about.

The clearest case, and the one that decides it: a desk that asks for a relative's details in order to proceed. A worker who learns that a user's mother is called such and such, and who can already see from the card that the task is a cardiology referral in a particular city, holds an identification. That task type is out of scope on this channel, and any other with the same shape is too.

1.3Where this is specified

The need-to-know filter is in 04, 1.1 and admits no name at all. The worked cards in 04, 1.4 state no name and carry the refusal line instead. The eligibility field in the party register is 02, 3.3, and a step type whose process needs a name spoken is not admitted to the catalogue for the phone surface at all.

2Rejected alternative(s) and their cost

A given name only, where the process needs one
The position this record replaces, and the one the worked cards carried until now. It reads as a small concession and it is not: a first name plus a city plus a condition is an identification, and the whole exposure this design exists to close is a worker being able to put a person to a set of circumstances. It also cannot be bounded, because every desk that asks for a name has a reason to, and the exception would widen by ordinary operational pressure.
An alias the worker reads out
Giving the worker a placeholder to say, with the party hearing the real value substituted in. It needs the media path DR-021 closed off, and it was closed off for good reasons that have not changed.
Vetting and the ledger as the closure
Accepting that a worker may hold a name, and relying on background checks, the need-to-know card, site controls and an access ledger to make misuse unattractive and detectable. That is a mitigation and not a mechanism, and the record's own rule is that a path is closed when a component property stops it. It would also be a promise the trust story could not honestly make.
Refusing calls altogether
The safest reading, and it costs the product the channel a great many Indian institutional desks are reachable on at all. The narrower exclusion here keeps calls where the party's process is already mapped and needs nothing further, which is most bookings, chasing and availability work.

3What this closes off

  • A name of any person on a card, a caption, a script line, a result field or a stored artefact a worker may view, in any form, including a given name alone and initials.
  • The given-name exception in the need-to-know filter, which is withdrawn.
  • A script line that asks a worker to state a name, or to confirm one a desk reads out.
  • A step type admitted to the phone surface whose party process needs a name spoken, or needs a relative's details, after the automated mapping.
  • Vetting, the card, site controls or the ledger offered as the closure for a name reaching a worker. They are the second line and this record is the first.
  • A task attempted with a careful script where the rule above would exclude it.

4Status

Fixed decision, taken by the founder on 2026-09-14, and absolute rather than staged. It closes the open item that carried the given-name exception, and it narrows the capability: the task types whose party process needs a name on a call leave the phone surface rather than moving to a workaround, and the roadmap's pilot scope loses them. Which task types those are is an operations question the party register answers party by party as its entries are drafted.

AI.fred is a personal AI that builds your context, task by task, until it can act in your personal life the way someone who has known you for years would. It handles the ordinary things, a table, a return, a rental search, a trip, and it is built so that it can also be trusted with the ones you dread: the bank, the visa, the claim, the admission. The dreaded tasks are why people come. The ordinary ones are why they stay, and why the context compounds fast enough to be worth anything. AI.fred is a working title. This publication is a design record, not an offer.
