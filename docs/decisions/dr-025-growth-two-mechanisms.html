DR-025Growth is organic and marketing, and the plan names both

Growth is the sum of two mechanisms rather than one exogenous rate: word of mouth compounding on the installed base, and a monthly media budget across Instagram reels, AI-influencer placements and the technology and business papers. A paid arrival joins the base that word of mouth compounds on, so marketing is the primer and not the engine.

On this page

1Decision

Growth is two mechanisms and the plan names both. Organic is word of mouth and referral: a share of the installed base each month, decaying as the easy audience is used up, against a market ceiling, and costing a referral incentive per arrival rather than a media rate. It is not a rate that arrives on its own: word of mouth at that level is conditional on the product being worth recommending, and the condition the founder sets is a net promoter score of 60 or above, measured on the paying cohort. Below it the organic mechanism is not assumed to hold and the media budget is not the remedy: a score under 60 is a product finding, and the answer to it is in the verdict system rather than in a reel. Marketing is a monthly budget converted into arrivals at a cost that rises as the budget does, across three channels: short video on Instagram bought on performance, AI-influencer placements bought per campaign, and coverage in the technology and business papers, which is earned rather than bought and therefore cheap and capped.

The two are not alternatives. A paid arrival joins the installed base, so word of mouth compounds on it in every later month: media buys a user and the users that user brings. That is why the modelled split moves the way it does, from about a quarter of arrivals bought in the first year to about a seventh by month 60, while the absolute media spend rises throughout. Marketing is the primer, not the engine. assumption

1.1The one number that decides whether to spend

What a paid arrival costs. Everything else follows from it, and nobody has run a reel, so it does not exist yet. What can be said without it is the shape. A user is worth about $1,291 after delivery cost on the published line, being $70 a month against a 4.3 per cent monthly churn and an 80 per cent delivery margin at month 36; on the organic-only floor the same arithmetic gives $1,172. At $120 a paid user the payback is under three months; at $250 it is five. assumption

Against the organic-only line this decision replaced, which needs 24.5 crore of peak cash on its median path and turns profitable in 89 paths out of a hundred, spending 15 per cent of revenue on media at $120 a paid user needs 15.0 crore, crosses over at month 25 rather than 40, and reaches 90,000 users rather than 30,000 by month 60. The paid cost at which that advantage disappears is about $1,124 a user at 15 per cent of revenue and about $671 at 25 per cent; at 5 per cent of revenue the cash need is never worse than organic alone, even at the $2,100 consumer-fintech benchmark the review carries. assumption

So the decision is to spend, and to spend modestly until the cost is measured: the first media budget is an instrument before it is a growth plan. Its trigger is in section 4.

1.2Where this is specified

The mechanism is in 05, 6, which carries the split, the break-even and the channels. The model that implements it is models/aifred_growth_model.py, which reproduces the published trajectory exactly with marketing switched off, so the two are comparable path by path rather than only in aggregate. The geography scenarios carry the same two mechanisms, in models/aifred_geo_growth.py, with the cost of a paid arrival abroad set by the ratio the geography model already held rather than by a new figure; the consequences are in 05, 8, and one of them, how a budget is allocated across live markets, is an open question rather than a decision.

2Rejected alternative(s) and their cost

Leaving growth exogenous
The position the model held before this record: a rate against the installed base with no spend causing an arrival. It owns a third of the variance of the cash need and 40 per cent of whether the business works at all, which makes the plan's largest variable the one it cannot influence. Keeping it would have meant a plan whose central number is a wish.
Paid acquisition as the engine
Buying the user base outright. The arithmetic says it does not work that way: the compounding sits in the organic mechanism, so beyond a modest share of revenue the extra spend buys arrivals the market ceiling then takes back, and the cash need stops improving. It also inverts the product's own argument, which is that a dread task landing properly is what makes someone tell a friend.
Performance channels only
Reels alone, on the grounds that they are measurable. Rejected because the product's proof is a story rather than a feature, and a technology or business paper carrying that story is the cheapest arrival in the mix and the one that reaches the people who have the tasks. It is capped, which is why it is a tenth of the budget rather than the whole of it.
A named CAC in the plan
Putting a cost per paid user into 05 as a figure. Rejected because there is no evidence for one: the earlier model's $13 an arrival was a referral cost rather than a media one, and the review's $1,340 to $2,140 is a US consumer fintech benchmark at a price this record does not charge. The plan carries the break-even instead, which is a claim that can be tested rather than a number that can only be believed. The $120 the mechanism takes as its input is the low-volume price of its cheapest channel, not a cost per user at scale: the run itself pays about $122 an arrival in the first year and about $1,515 by month 60, which is inside that benchmark, and 05, 6.3 states it.

3What this closes off

  • A trajectory in 05 whose growth has no mechanism behind it.
  • A media budget without a measured cost per arrival behind it, beyond the first budget, whose purpose is to measure exactly that.
  • Paid acquisition presented as the growth engine, or organic presented as the whole of it.
  • A channel added to the mix without its own cost, its own saturation and its own place in the split.
  • A cost per acquired user quoted anywhere in this publication as though it were evidence.

4Status

Fixed decision, taken by the founder on 2026-09-14. The mechanism is fixed; every number behind the marketing half is a prior, and the first of them to be replaced is the cost per paid arrival. Its trigger is the first media spend of the pilot, which is why it sits in 07 with the founder as owner and a two-week instrument rather than a milestone behind it.

AI.fred is a personal AI that builds your context, task by task, until it can act in your personal life the way someone who has known you for years would. It handles the ordinary things, a table, a return, a rental search, a trip, and it is built so that it can also be trusted with the ones you dread: the bank, the visa, the claim, the admission. The dreaded tasks are why people come. The ordinary ones are why they stay, and why the context compounds fast enough to be worth anything. AI.fred is a working title. This publication is a design record, not an offer.
