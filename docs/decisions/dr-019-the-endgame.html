On this page

1Decision

The endgame described in the previous record is the starting scope, not a later phase. AI.fred acts across the whole of a user's personal economic life from the first day, and builds a context profile as it does so (00, 2). The high-consequence tasks are the acquisition hook and the proof of trustworthiness; they are not the boundary of what the product does, and DR-020 states the scope in its own terms.

The previous record gated everyday tasks on legal packs opening in each market. That gate was the wrong way round: the legal packs govern identity and institutional acts, which are the consequential tasks, not the everyday ones. A table booking waits on nothing.

1.1On data as a business

The previous record contemplated a second shape of company, monetising accumulated user data once the base was large enough. That is withdrawn. Three distinct things sat inside it and only two survive.

Data used to improve the product is the highest-value use by a wide margin and starts early, at roughly two to three thousand users with six months of history, because stable archetype segments need a few hundred users each. assumption It feeds automation share, which is the variable the whole business rests on, and 05 carries the arithmetic with the model page behind it.

Data used as commerce is real: a take rate on transactions the user already asked us to execute, worth roughly 1.2 per cent of routed spend, meaningful from about four hundred subscribers on the model's own routed spend per user. assumption It is bound by one rule that is not negotiable, recorded in DR-024: a merchant may never pay for placement in a recommendation.

Data sold as an asset is withdrawn entirely. Consumer panels monetise at roughly $10 to $40 per panellist a year, so fifty thousand users is half a million to two million dollars a year, which against the same base at the published line's revenue per user is between one and five per cent of subscription revenue. assumption Against that, the business is acutely churn-sensitive, and a one point rise in monthly churn at that size costs several times what the data would earn. Purpose limitation under the DPDP Act and the GDPR means the consent we hold for executing tasks does not extend to producing market intelligence, and anonymisation is hard to defend when the underlying records include identity documents. Crossing into significant-data-fiduciary territory adds obligations that grow with the user count rather than shrinking. The trade is bad on every axis at once.

2Rejected alternative(s) and their cost

A staged product, three or four hard tasks a year first
The previous record's order. Its cost is that a profile built on that cadence never compounds, a habit cannot form on that frequency, and the value proposition that justifies a subscription is availability rather than occasional rescue.
Data sold as an asset once the base is large enough
Withdrawn for the reasons in 1.1: it earns between one and five per cent of subscription revenue against a churn exposure several times larger, and the consent we hold does not reach it. assumption
Deciding nothing and leaving the data story open
The previous record's position. Its cost is that an investor reads the open item as a plan, and the privacy posture 03 records cannot be defended while a second customer is contemplated.

3What this closes off

  • The claim that nobody serves this corner of the market. On the everyday layer we sit beside the flat-fee assistants and the free platform agents, and the argument for the price is carried by the context profile and by access to the consequential tail.
  • A legal-pack gate on everyday work anywhere in the record.
  • A data-as-asset revenue line, a panel, or any sale of user data or insight derived from it, in any market and at any scale.
  • A merchant paying for placement in a recommendation, which DR-024 states as its own rule.
  • The staged order of the previous record, and with it the argument that the wedge is the boundary rather than the entry.

4Status

Fixed decision, taken by the founder on 2026-09-13, superseding DR-019 as published on 2026-09-10. Its scope half is a decision; its data half rests on the modelled priors of 05, which is why this page carries the assumption chip and the page status follows the record's precedence rather than the word decision. DR-020 carries the scope, DR-024 the pricing rule, DR-016 the store the profile is held in, unchanged, and DR-015 is withdrawn with the deferral it recorded.

AI.fred is a personal AI that builds your context, task by task, until it can act in your personal life the way someone who has known you for years would. It handles the ordinary things, a table, a return, a rental search, a trip, and it is built so that it can also be trusted with the ones you dread: the bank, the visa, the claim, the admission. The dreaded tasks are why people come. The ordinary ones are why they stay, and why the context compounds fast enough to be worth anything. AI.fred is a working title. This publication is a design record, not an offer.
