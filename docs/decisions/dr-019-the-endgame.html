On this page

1Decision

The wedge is the entry and the proof; the destination is the user's first port of call for everything personal. AI.fred is an AI that builds the user's context, task by task, and uses it to do in the user's personal life what the general assistants do for everyone, and that can be trusted with the high-value, low-volume tasks people dread a few times a year: the ones that carry real consequence. The order is fixed: the wedge first, because it is where trust is proven and where the context that makes everything else possible is earned; the everyday tasks next, on the same harness; the first port of call as the result, not a launch. The value to the user stays front and centre. The asset behind it is the context store DR-016 fixes, the fullest and most trusted picture of a person's affairs, held for that person. What that asset could be worth beyond the user's own tasks is real upside and the hook for investment; it depends on the user proposition succeeding first, and it is a story for a later time, named here as potential and decided by nobody yet.

1.1The sequence

Everyday tasks run on the harness built for the wedge: the same envelope, the same aliases, the same ledger and the same classifier. What changes is what the classifier finds: most everyday steps are reversible, so they run as ai steps with no worker and no dual control, and the point-of-no-return test answers yes at once. The thesis's argument against the generalist stands, because it was never about how many kinds of task an assistant takes; it was about an assistant that sees everything. Ours takes more and sees nothing. A market opens its everyday verticals by a line of its legal pack after the pilot's gate, as it opens any vertical, and not by a build; until then an everyday request is refused as not offered, never as too small. The everyday product is not launched first anywhere: the wedge proves the trust architecture in a market before that market's pack opens the rest.

1.2The asset

Every task teaches the store a little more, with source, date and confidence, and the store is what lets the next task, and the everyday ones, run with fewer questions and more confidence. That accumulation is why the first port of call is a result and not a positioning: the assistant that already holds the person's affairs, with provenance the person can audit, is the one they turn to first. The store is held for the user, under the purpose 03 records, the task and its envelope, and the eight moat properties of 00 are unchanged by this record; the store is the asset that compounds behind them.

1.3The upside, left open

A company that holds, for millions of people, the most complete and most trusted picture of their affairs holds something of value beyond each person's own tasks. This record names that upside because an investor will, and because the founder holds it as the hook, and it decides nothing about it: what may be done with the store beyond the user's own tasks, if anything, is an open item in 07 with the founder as its owner, triggered when 05 reopens after a Series A or B or by the first investor conversation that needs an answer, and it is settled only by a decision record, with its rejected alternatives and its closes-off list, never by a line elsewhere. Until then the record carries no commitment either way, and the purpose statement in 03 stands as written.

1.4Where it is specified

The premise is section 6 of 00; the everyday tasks' place beside the taxonomy is in 01, 2 and the refusal that holds until a pack opens them in 01, 7; the store in 02 and DR-016; the legal pack's line in 06, 4.2; the open item in 07; the economics deferral in 05 and DR-015.

2Rejected alternative(s) and their cost

Staying a high-consequence specialist
A concierge for the dreaded tasks and nothing else. Its cost is a ceiling: a few tasks a year form no habit, the context store fills slowly and mostly with institutional facts, and the first port of call is somebody else.
Launching as a general assistant first
The flat-fee players' ground and the labs' ground, with no trust proof and no reason to be chosen; the thesis's own argument, that a generalist who sees everything cannot be trusted with the wedge, would apply to us.
Deciding the data story now, either way
A commitment to a data business would put a second customer beside the user before the first has been served, and would prejudice the privacy posture 03 records; a closure would foreclose an upside before anyone knows its shape. Both are decisions for a later record.
A separate everyday product or brand
Two harnesses, two trust stories and a context split in two; the store's value is that it is one.

3What this closes off

  • A second harness, or a lighter trust rule, for everyday tasks: the same envelope, aliases, ledger and classifier, with the classifier finding reversible steps.
  • A task refused for being everyday once a market's legal pack opens its everyday verticals.
  • The everyday product launched in a market before the wedge has proven the trust architecture there.
  • A use of the context store beyond the user's own tasks decided anywhere but by a decision record when the open item in 07 resolves.
  • A revenue, pricing or valuation claim for the data or for the general assistant anywhere but 05, when DR-015 reopens.
  • A change to the eight moat properties or to the wedge on the strength of this record.
