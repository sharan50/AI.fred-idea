4The numbers that matter

05 Business, 3 The one ratio that decides everything

Delivery cost scales with usage, not with users. The governing number is therefore human minutes per user per month, which is the product of promised task volume and blended handling time, set against average revenue per user.

05 Business, 2 The task model

Every task resolves into one of five outcomes, and the mix is what sets the cost base.

05 Business, 5 The trajectory

Twenty thousand simulated paths, sixty months, every driver sampled rather than assumed. Figure 5.2 is the planning line itself, revenue against the whole cost base; Figure 5.3 is the ratio the levers move; Figure 5.4 is the cash the trough asks for. Conservative and optimistic lines are drawn from coherent families of paths, ranked by outcome, rather than by blending unrelated percentiles. The planning line weights them 65 to 35, conservative to optimistic.

05 Business, 8 Geography

Purchasing power is the argument for expansion and it holds: a US user paying $105 costs the same to serve as an Indian user paying $52, because the delivery floor is in Bengaluru either way. assumption

06 Roadmap, 3 Build versus buy

We buy what must be boring and audited and build what carries the promise: four components bought, sixteen built. Table 6.1 gives the reason for each and what the choice commits us to, since buying is a commitment to someone else's roadmap, regions and outages.

Measurement protocol, 1 What it measures

Three estimated numbers become three measured ones, at a cost of about a week of one person's time and a few hundred dollars of interface spend, before any floor is leased or any engine is chosen.

01 Product, 5 The verdict system

Two loops, two jobs. The control loop decides whether a task can be shown as done; the improvement loop decides whether it was done well, and only the user can say. Keeping them apart is DR-002: an outcome code is not ground truth of quality, and a verdict is not a gate on execution.

AI.fred is a personal AI that builds your context, task by task, until it can act in your personal life the way someone who has known you for years would. It handles the ordinary things, a table, a return, a rental search, a trip, and it is built so that it can also be trusted with the ones you dread: the bank, the visa, the claim, the admission. The dreaded tasks are why people come. The ordinary ones are why they stay, and why the context compounds fast enough to be worth anything. AI.fred is a working title. This publication is a design record, not an offer.
