1The harness-first build sequence

We build the harness in the order the two fatal failures dictate, before anything a customer can see. The alias vault and the substitutor come first, so no later component ever handles a real value. The envelope and the classifier come second, so no later executor can fire a step outside its grant. The state machine and the watchdog come third, so no task can be forgotten once tasks exist. Only then do we build a surface a step can run on. Figure 6.1 draws the eight stages with what each makes possible.

The order is set by preconditions, not the calendar. Each stage has a gate, a test an engineer can automate, and no stage starts before the previous gate has passed. No dates appear here: a stage that has not passed its gate is not late, it is unfinished. The demo order, channel and browser agent first with the vault bolted on later, is rejected: every surface and every log built before the alias rule would have held real values, and the threat model in 03 would have to close paths that need never have existed.

Stage 1: alias vault and substitution
Precondition: a vault vendor chosen against the six tests in DR-008, an item in 07. The vault is bought and the substitutor is written: the one small service with detokenise rights, in its own environment, injecting directly into a surface on the interface 02 fixes and never returning a value. Three more pieces ship with it: the alias grammar of 03 ({{pan}}, {{account.hdfc}}, {{card.primary.last4}}); the vault's edge proxy, which the provider runs in the market's region under our detector policy and at which the channel webhook, the device form and every rail response terminate, so that the intake gateway and the passport adapters of stage 8 receive aliases and never hold a raw byte; and the hash service, the one vault-facing interface of ours outside the substitutor, which holds the digest keys, issues the two keyed digest sets 03 specifies, one to the maskers and one to the verifier, and digests each candidate window a masker sends over a batched, rate-limited call, never a value and never a detokenise right. The third door, a third party echoing a value back, is closed on each surface by maskers built against that service as stages 4 and 5 build it. Gate: the vault SDK is absent from every build artefact except the substitutor and the hash service, checked by the build; the gateway and the adapters hold no vault SDK, no vault credential and no network route to the vault; nothing else can detokenise; the substitutor writes a placement line on every call; and the proxy contract, bytes in and aliases out under our detectors, is exercised against a stub so that a second provider can be held to it.
Stage 2: envelope, classifier and planner
The envelope schema with its five fields, the point-of-no-return test, the rule of classification, and the policy layer as a loaded flag table keyed by jurisdiction and act type, holding all three markets' drafts with a condition line where one applies and a missing row evaluating to bounce, consulted once under the delegator's jurisdiction and, for an act toward an institution, once more under the institution's market, the stricter result governing; the first market's draft is replaced by its signed table before the pilot opens. The step-type catalogue of 02 is built here: its schema is core, and its entries for the pilot's task types carry each step type's act type, default class, reversibility, executor classes, surfaces, alias classes, evidence expected, bounce form, allowed response windows and delay-line length, and no policy flag of its own, because the flag lives in the policy layer. The planner is built here as a schema-validated model call over the catalogue, proposing an envelope with every plan. It reads facts through the context store's read interface, fixed in this stage and served from fixtures, facts in the schema of DR-016 loaded from the evaluation set; stage 3 puts the store behind the same interface, so nothing built here waits on it. Gate, in three parts. The catalogue's entries for the pilot's task types are complete, every entry carrying a class and a reversibility. The classifier evaluation set passes in full: a table of cases, each a step record as 02 defines it without its class, reversibility and route, with the envelope version, the market's flag table and the fixture facts it is judged against and the expected class, reversibility and route written by a person; one case per step in each pilot task type's plan shape plus the edge cases (outside the envelope, past the validity window, flagged bounce, unknown step type); exact agreement is the pass, and an undecidable case must come out as bounce. The planner evaluation set passes: per task type, normalised requests in 02's request format with fixture facts and, for each, the expected plan shape, the step types in order with their class and reversibility, the point of no return and the envelope proposal's five fields, scored by exact match on shape and envelope; that is the standard until verdicts exist, and the set verdict rates join once they do. And a check in the substitutor and every executor refuses any step outside the envelope. The envelope a user sees in 01 is the object the harness enforces.
Stage 3: state machine and watchdog
The states, transitions, timers and nudges of the state machine in 02, lapsed as a state only the system assigns, and the watchdog that feeds the exceptions board. The context store of DR-016 is built here, behind the read interface stage 2 fixed, because facts accrue once tasks exist: every fact carries its source, date, validity interval, confidence and whether the user confirmed it, and sensitive fields are aliases. Gate: the invariant survives fault injection (delete a timer; the task must reach the board before anything else happens); every transition is recorded with its cause; no code path reaches a terminal state without one; the store rejects a fact without its provenance fields. Tasks can now exist, synthetic ones until stage 8 admits a real user: a task can go quiet safely, and we cannot forget.
Stage 4: the managed browser
The first execution surface, and the accent in the figure: our browser and its controller, the first of the three surface controllers that alone may request a placement; the substitutor's driver typing the value into the field; the worker's stream rendering it as dots; stream and DOM capture masked by keyed digest against the hash service. The evidence pipeline (capture, redact, verify, store) ships inside this stage, the first moment a real value exists outside the vault. Gate: a canary test plants a known value and proves it appears in no stored artefact, no log and no frame a worker can see; the verify pass fails closed. The surface for ai steps on any web form, most of the wedge, now exists and is exercised against synthetic tasks under a test envelope; no human step runs before the console of stage 6, and no step of a real user's task before the device surface of stage 8 has confirmed an envelope, as DR-004 closes off.
Stage 5: telephony and mail relay
Our numbers on a bought carrier platform, one call leg per party, with no value ever placed on a call in either direction and no media server, no mute, no delay line and no re-voicing to build (DR-021); the stage is correspondingly smaller, and what remains of it is dialling under the controller, keypad navigation under the step's menu map, and both legs delivered to the evidence pipeline. The mail relay is unchanged. The masked stream of stage 4 is the fallback for an unmodelled counterparty (DR-022) and is deferred with it, so what stage 4 builds is the structured task card and the library behind it. Formerly: the inbound leg behind a delay line at all times, muting an echoed value or a class-shaped run before the worker hears it; transcripts alias-replaced before storage; the relay swapping aliases into outbound mail after composition. The telephony controller and the relay are the second and third surface controllers, and with the browser controller they are the only writers of a placement request. Gate: the canary test repeated on audio, transcript and mail; the provider records, transcribes and stores nothing, checked by configuration audit; a recording that fails the verify pass is destroyed, never stored; the relay holds no raw value at rest; the send scope proven against the launch market's leading mail providers and the draft route exercised end to end.
Stage 6: worker console
The task card, alias-only rendering, the outcome-code vocabulary, the dual-control check and the exceptions board, as the worker console page specifies them; the console never calls the substitutor, and a tap on Place goes to the task service and on to the step's surface controller. Gate: no vault SDK in the console and no route from it to the substitutor; the stream, DOM capture and transcript arrive already masked by the stage 4 and 5 gates and the console has no other input; copy, download, print and screenshot disabled; the canary test repeated through every panel; the invariant identical to 02; the L1 pool has run a shift on it against synthetic tasks.
Stage 7: the access ledger
The entry format and the substitutor's write have existed since stage 1; entries accumulate from the first placement in stage 4. This stage renders them per task, in plain words, as the ledger specification in 03 sets out, in a view the device surface of stage 8 carries. Gate: every placement line maps to one substitutor call; view and exposure lines are written by the surface or the evidence store and reference the placement they relate to; the ledger holds aliases and never values, tested against the threat model's evidence paths; a user reads a task's ledger without asking us. The trust story becomes something a customer checks.
Stage 8: gateway, device surface and first adapters
Precondition: the first market chosen, the amber item of section 2.5. The channel-agnostic gateway, behind the vault's edge proxy of stage 1, normalising every channel into one request format with aliases already in place; the user's authenticated device surface, carrying envelope confirmation, extension approval, bounce, outcome report, verdict and the ledger view, with the universal device bounce-back; the first market's passport adapters, behind the same proxy, receiving aliases only. The front door is the per-market choice of DR-001. Gate: a value typed, uploaded or spoken by a user is an alias before the gateway, let alone the planner, holds a byte; an adapter never holds a raw byte and has no route to the vault; the bounce-back works with no adapter; no envelope confirmation or bounce approval is accepted from a channel that authenticates only a phone number. A real user can now arrive, and the pilot can open.

2Pilot scope in capability terms

The pilot is scoped by what the harness can do safely, not by how many people use it or what they pay. Five things define it: verticals, step classes and reversibility, delegated identity acts, surfaces, and the market it opens in. The first four are proposed here and the sequence of verticals is amber in 07, as is the market.

2.1Verticals

Three of the six marquee verticals open the pilot as proposed here: banking and wealth, identity and security, and legal and administrative. Every task in them exercises the substitutor and the envelope, so the pilot tests the promise itself and not a proxy. Health and medical joins when an L2 health pod is staffed on the shift that holds the pilot market, because health tasks live on booking lines and a pod that knows them is the precondition; the telephony gate is passed before the pilot opens (2.4), so the pod, not the surface, is what the vertical waits for. Travel and family affairs join last: they carry less consequence, sit in the flat-fee category of the landscape in 00, and are on the roadmap because a household delegates all its admin to one assistant, not because they prove anything about trust. That sequence is proposed, not fixed: it is the founder's item in 07, with the scoping of this pilot as its trigger amber.

2.2Step classes and reversibility

In the pilot, ai steps run only on the managed browser; human steps run from task cards on every surface; user steps bounce to the user's device without exception. Those restrictions live in one place, the scope block of the first market's legal pack, which section 3.1 of 02 fixes: it names the surfaces open to ai steps (the managed browser alone), whether the harness may compose mail (off, so the composition 02 permits is switched off for the pilot as well as the execution), the pool that may execute an irreversible human step (L3 alone) and the verticals open. The classifier and the routing table read the block before any catalogue entry and never this page, so a step that would be ai on a closed surface comes out human without a special case, and lifting a restriction after the pilot is a signed reload of the pack, not a code change. Reversible steps inside the envelope execute. For an irreversible step inside the envelope, whichever party executes, the other checks before it fires: when the AI executes, an L3 worker holds the checking seat; when a worker executes, the AI checks the step against the envelope and the expected outcome, as 04 and DR-004 fix it. Human execution of irreversible steps is confined to L3 for the pilot by the scope block's line, which the routing table 04 owns reads at card issue; the rule is a line of the legal pack, neither a catalogue entry nor a setting of the table itself. Irreversible steps outside the envelope bounce; reversible steps outside it become extension requests. Envelope defaults start narrow and are widened by the user deliberately.

2.3Delegated identity acts

Only the identity act types the first market's policy layer flags as delegated are delegated; everything flagged bounce or prohibited stays with the user even where the user ticks it. Two tables are in play and the difference is deliberate: Table 3.5 in 03 records the designed flag per jurisdiction and act type, the ceiling, every cell to-verify; the signed table the pilot runs under is the legal pack's, and for the UK and the US it sets every identity act to bounce until counsel's answer sets the flag, whatever the designed cell reads, and it sets make a payment under the cap to bounce in every market, so the pilot moves no money on the user's behalf, as 07 records. The flag table is the first draft of that market's lobbying agenda (the policy flags in 03), and every prohibited line is a legal to-verify with an owner in 07.

2.4Surfaces

All three, from the first task: the managed browser, our telephony and the mail relay have each passed their gate before the pilot opens. A worker never uses a tool of their own, and the refusals in 01 apply unchanged.

2.5Which market first

The pilot is delivered from India, because that is where the floor, the library and the flag table with delegated identity rows are, and which market the first cohort lives in is the amber item of section 2.5; the commercial sequence beyond it is section 4.1 and DR-023, with the United Kingdom between months 8 and 14 amber. What the decision changes is bounded: which of the three flag tables the pilot runs under, which adapters stage 8 builds first, the legal pack, the shift on the wheel and the front door of DR-001. It changes neither the order of the eight stages nor their gates; the harness is built in Bengaluru whichever way it goes, as the markets section of 00 sets out. One build, three possible first doors.

What the pilot must produce is fixed too: verdict rates per task type joined to step traces, the improvement loop of DR-002; the exceptions-board history; a ledger for every task; and the step trace and verdict record from which the second and third inputs of 05 fall out of operation rather than estimation. The pilot opens on three conditions: every gate passed; the first market's legal pack complete, meaning a signed flag table, keyed by jurisdiction and act type, with every line marked delegated, bounce or prohibited, its condition where one applies, and a legal item behind every prohibited line, the market's residency-map entry and its refusals list; and a person who did not build the harness having hunted the two fatals through it and found nothing.

4The country-expansion playbook

A new country is an adapter, a legal pack and a shift, and nothing else changes: that is DR-007, the universal core as one country-agnostic build that entering a country must not touch. The checklist below runs for each market. A country is live when every line is ticked, not before.

4.1The sequence, and the gates

The sequence is not the one the previous edition carried, and the reason is arithmetic rather than ambition. The United Kingdom opens between months 8 and 14, with acquisition genuinely moved there rather than treated as secondary, because the modelling says a late, secondary expansion is worse than not expanding at all: the standing cost of a foreign entity arrives long before its users do, which nearly triples the conservative cash requirement while lowering the probability of profitability (assumption 05, 8). India is then the delivery base, the workflow laboratory and a secondary revenue market, not the first of three equal markets: the floor, the library and the measurement all live there, and its revenue is real but is not what the plan turns on.

Two gates govern any market, and they are gates rather than forecasts: 400 users in the United Kingdom and 600 in the United States to carry the local cost base. No market is opened that cannot plausibly reach 500 users within twelve months of entry, and no second market is opened until the first has cleared its gate. Below the gate a market is a drag; above it each user is the purchasing-power arbitrage the expansion is for. DR-023 fixes the sequence and the gates, and is amber amber: its trigger is a priced United Kingdom waiting list aimed at non-resident households, measured for conversion, because the case rests on acquiring abroad from a standing start with no brand and no local team.

The United States follows the same test rather than a date, and the playbook below is what either market has to absorb.

4.2The adapter

  1. Inventory the country's rails: identity, documents, bank data, payments, signatures, and the institutions the wedge touches.
  2. For each rail, choose one of three routes: direct where the rail admits us; through a regulated partner where it is reserved to regulated entities; bounce to the user's device where neither is open.
  3. Build the passport adapter behind the boundary the adapter model in 02 draws: the rail's response terminates at the vault's edge proxy in front of it, so the adapter receives aliases only and holds no raw byte.
  4. Pass adapter conformance: a planted value cannot cross the boundary; every consent artefact and its expiry is a dated fact in the context store.

4.3The legal pack

The pack's verticals line governs identity and institutional acts, which are the consequential tasks; the everyday work is in scope from the first day and waits on no pack, as DR-019 and DR-020 record.

  1. Load the policy flag table, keyed by jurisdiction and act type: delegated, bounce, or prohibited pending a regulatory answer, with a condition line where one applies; a missing row evaluates to bounce; every line that is not delegated becomes a lobbying-agenda entry with its end-user benefit stated.
  2. File the KYC last-mile constraints as legal to-verifies with owners in 07. Design carries the ceiling; the flags carry the law.
  3. Add the country to the data-residency map: vault, evidence-store and inference regions, and the safeguards for Bengaluru staff processing its data, for the UK a restricted cross-border transfer needing contractual safeguards to-verify.
  4. Extend the privacy-law skeleton in 03 and record the consumer-law liability position: it is our name on the agent.
  5. Settle the contactable-party defaults and the refusals list for the country.

4.4The shift

  1. Place the country on the 24/7 wheel in 04: the shift covering its business hours and the handovers either side.
  2. Stand up the L2 pods for the verticals opening there, with QA and escalation cover on the same shift.
  3. Provision numbers, caller identity and languages on our telephony, and the substitution stations under the site controls, which do not vary by country.
  4. Render the ledger in the country's plain words and check that the trust story reads true there.

The three markets are instances of the same list, with preconditions the research to date states only partly. Every to-verify line has an owner in 07. AI.fred is a personal AI that builds your context, task by task, until it can act in your personal life the way someone who has known you for years would. It handles the ordinary things, a table, a return, a rental search, a trip, and it is built so that it can also be trusted with the ones you dread: the bank, the visa, the claim, the admission. The dreaded tasks are why people come. The ordinary ones are why they stay, and why the context compounds fast enough to be worth anything. AI.fred is a working title. This publication is a design record, not an offer.
