1The harness-first build sequence

We build the harness in the order the two fatal failures dictate, before anything a customer can see. The alias vault and the substitutor come first, so no later component ever handles a real value. The envelope and the classifier come second, so no later executor can fire a step outside its grant. The state machine and the watchdog come third, so no task can be forgotten once tasks exist. Only then do we build a surface a step can run on. Figure 6.1 draws the eight stages with what each makes possible.

The order is set by preconditions, not the calendar. Each stage has a gate, a test an engineer can automate, and no stage starts before the previous gate has passed. No dates appear here: a stage that has not passed its gate is not late, it is unfinished. The demo order, channel and browser agent first with the vault bolted on later, is rejected: every surface and every log built before the alias rule would have held real values, and the threat model in 03 would have to close paths that need never have existed.

Stage 1: alias vault and substitution
Precondition: a vault vendor chosen against the five tests in DR-008, an item in 07. The vault is bought and the substitutor is written: the one small service with detokenise rights, in its own environment, injecting directly into a surface on the interface 02 fixes and never returning a value. Three more pieces ship with it: the alias grammar ({{pan}}, {{account.hdfc}}); the two tokenising edges, intake and adapter boundary; and the hash service, which serves hashes and match-or-no-match answers, never a value, the only vault-facing interface outside the substitutor and the edges. The third entry route, a third party echoing a value back, is masked against that service by each surface as stages 4 and 5 build it. Gate: the vault SDK is absent from every build artefact except the substitutor, the two edges and the hash service, checked by the build; nothing else can detokenise; the substitutor writes a placement line on every call.
Stage 2: envelope, classifier and planner
The envelope schema with its five fields, the point-of-no-return test, the rule of classification, and the policy layer as a loaded flag table holding all three markets' drafts; the first market's draft is replaced by its signed table before the pilot opens. The planner is built here as a schema-validated model call over the step library, proposing an envelope with every plan and reading the context store from stage 3. Gate: a step library for the pilot's task types in which every step carries a class and a reversibility; a classifier evaluation set; a check in the substitutor and every executor that refuses any step outside the envelope. The envelope a user sees in 01 is the object the harness enforces.
Stage 3: state machine and watchdog
The states, transitions, timers and nudges of the state machine in 02, lapsed as a state only the system assigns, and the watchdog that feeds the exceptions board. The context store of DR-016 is built here, because facts accrue once tasks exist: every fact carries its source, date, validity interval, confidence and whether the user confirmed it, and sensitive fields are aliases. Gate: the invariant survives fault injection (delete a timer; the task must reach the board before anything else happens); every transition is recorded with its cause; no code path reaches a terminal state without one; the store rejects a fact without its provenance fields. Tasks can now exist: a user can go quiet safely, and we cannot forget.
Stage 4: the managed browser
The first execution surface, and the accent in the figure: our browser, the substitutor's driver typing the value into the field, the worker's stream rendering it as dots, stream and DOM capture masked by known-value matching against the hash service. The evidence pipeline (capture, redact, verify, store) ships inside this stage, the first moment a real value exists outside the vault. Gate: a canary test plants a known value and proves it appears in no stored artefact, no log and no frame a worker can see; the verify pass fails closed. ai and human steps can now run on any web form, most of the wedge.
Stage 5: telephony and mail relay
Our numbers on a bought carrier platform, one call leg per party, the value spoken or keyed on the far leg while the worker's leg is muted and their panel shows "value placed"; inbound audio through the delay buffer that mutes an echoed value; transcripts alias-replaced before storage; the relay swapping aliases into outbound mail after composition. Gate: the canary test repeated on audio, transcript and mail; the provider records, transcribes and stores nothing, checked by configuration audit; a recording that fails the verify pass is destroyed, never stored; the relay holds no raw value at rest.
Stage 6: worker console
The task card, alias-only rendering, the outcome-code vocabulary, the dual-control check and the exceptions board, as the worker console page specifies them. Gate: no vault SDK in the console; the stream, DOM capture and transcript arrive already masked by the stage 4 and 5 gates and the console has no other input; copy, download, print and screenshot disabled; the canary test repeated through every panel; the invariant identical to 02; the L1 pool has run a shift on it against synthetic tasks.
Stage 7: the access ledger
The entry format and the substitutor's write have existed since stage 1; entries accumulate from the first placement in stage 4. This stage renders them per task, in plain words, as the ledger specification in 03 sets out, in a view the device surface of stage 8 carries. Gate: every placement line maps to one substitutor call; view and mute lines are written by the surface or the evidence store and reference the placement they relate to; the ledger holds aliases and never values, tested against the threat model's evidence paths; a user reads a task's ledger without asking us. The trust story becomes something a customer checks.
Stage 8: gateway, device surface and first adapters
Precondition: the first market chosen, the amber item of section 2.5. The channel-agnostic gateway normalising every channel into one request format and tokenising any typed or uploaded value at the edge; the user's authenticated device surface, carrying envelope confirmation, extension approval, bounce, outcome report, verdict and the ledger view, with the universal device bounce-back; the first market's passport adapters, tokenising at their boundary. The front door is the per-market choice of DR-001. Gate: a value typed by a user is an alias before the planner sees it; an adapter cannot return a raw value; the bounce-back works with no adapter; no envelope confirmation or bounce approval is accepted from a channel that authenticates only a phone number. A real user can now arrive, and the pilot can open.

2Pilot scope in capability terms

The pilot is scoped by what the harness can do safely, not by how many people use it or what they pay. Five things define it: verticals, step classes and reversibility, delegated identity acts, surfaces, and the market it opens in. The first four are fixed here; the fifth is amber.

2.1Verticals

Three of the six marquee verticals open the pilot: banking and wealth, identity and security, and legal and administrative. Every task in them exercises the substitutor and the envelope, so the pilot tests the promise itself and not a proxy. Health and medical joins when an L2 health pod is staffed, because health tasks live on booking lines. Travel and family affairs join last: they carry less consequence, sit in the flat-fee category of the landscape in 00, and are on the roadmap because a household delegates all its admin to one assistant, not because they prove anything about trust.

2.2Step classes and reversibility

In the pilot, ai steps run only on the managed browser; human steps run from task cards on every surface; user steps bounce to the user's device without exception. Outbound mail is composed by workers only: harness composition, which 02 permits, is switched off for the pilot, so the restriction covers composition as well as execution. Reversible steps inside the envelope execute. For an irreversible step inside the envelope, whichever party executes, the other checks before it fires: when the AI executes, an L3 worker holds the checking seat; when a worker executes, the AI checks the step against the envelope and the expected outcome, as 04 and DR-004 fix it. Human execution of irreversible steps is confined to L3 for the pilot. Irreversible steps outside the envelope bounce; reversible steps outside it become extension requests. Envelope defaults start narrow and are widened by the user deliberately.

2.3Delegated identity acts

Only the identity act types the first market's policy layer flags as delegated are delegated; everything flagged bounce or prohibited stays with the user even where the user ticks it. The flag table is the first draft of that market's lobbying agenda (the policy flags in 03), and every prohibited line is a legal to-verify with an owner in 07.

2.4Surfaces

All three, from the first task: the managed browser, our telephony and the mail relay have each passed their gate before the pilot opens. A worker never uses a tool of their own, and the refusals in 01 apply unchanged.

2.5Which market first

The first market is amber: the item sits in 07, owned by the founder, with the scoping of this pilot as its trigger, and this page does not pre-empt it. What the decision changes is bounded: which of the three flag tables the pilot runs under, which adapters stage 8 builds first, the legal pack, the shift on the wheel and the front door of DR-001. It changes neither the order of the eight stages nor their gates; the harness is built in Bengaluru whichever way it goes, as the markets section of 00 sets out. One build, three possible first doors.

What the pilot must produce is fixed too: verdict rates per task type joined to step traces, the improvement loop of DR-002; the exceptions-board history; a ledger for every task; and the step trace and verdict record from which the second and third inputs of 05 fall out of operation rather than estimation. The pilot opens on three conditions: every gate passed; the first market's legal pack complete, meaning a signed flag table with every step type marked delegated, bounce or prohibited and a legal item behind every prohibited line, the market's residency-map entry and its refusals list; and a person who did not build the harness having hunted the two fatals through it and found nothing.

4The country-expansion playbook

A new country is an adapter, a legal pack and a shift, and nothing else changes: that is DR-007, the universal core as one country-agnostic build that entering a country must not touch. The checklist below runs for each of the three markets and any fourth. A country is live when every line is ticked, not before.

4.1The adapter

  1. Inventory the country's rails: identity, documents, bank data, payments, signatures, and the institutions the wedge touches.
  2. For each rail, choose one of three routes: direct where the rail admits us; through a regulated partner where it is reserved to regulated entities; bounce to the user's device where neither is open.
  3. Build the passport adapter behind the boundary the adapter model in 02 draws: it tokenises every value it receives and returns aliases only.
  4. Pass adapter conformance: a planted value cannot cross the boundary; every consent artefact and its expiry is a dated fact in the context store.

4.2The legal pack

  1. Load the policy flag table: per step type, delegated, bounce, or prohibited pending a regulatory answer; every prohibited line becomes a lobbying-agenda entry with its end-user benefit stated.
  2. File the KYC last-mile constraints as legal to-verifies with owners in 07. Design carries the ceiling; the flags carry the law.
  3. Add the country to the data-residency map: vault, evidence-store and inference regions, and the safeguards for Bengaluru staff processing its data, for the UK a restricted cross-border transfer needing contractual safeguards to-verify.
  4. Extend the privacy-law skeleton in 03 and record the consumer-law liability position: it is our name on the agent.
  5. Settle the contactable-party defaults and the refusals list for the country.

4.3The shift

  1. Place the country on the 24/7 wheel in 04: the shift covering its business hours and the handovers either side.
  2. Stand up the L2 pods for the verticals opening there, with QA and escalation cover on the same shift.
  3. Provision numbers, caller identity and languages on our telephony, and the substitution stations under the site controls, which do not vary by country.
  4. Render the ledger in the country's plain words and check that the trust story reads true there.

The three markets are instances of the same list, with preconditions the research to date states only partly. Table 6.2 records the lines that decide an adapter's route or a legal pack's contents, chipped as the evidence allows; what else the rails permit lives in the passports in 03. Every to-verify line has an owner in 07. The KYC last mile in the UK and the US, UK identity rails and US bank-data aggregation have not been researched at all, and the table does not pretend otherwise.
