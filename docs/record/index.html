1What the build record is

The record is everything that was checked before a page was allowed to stand, kept where a reader can follow it. The full review reads the venture as a partner would, from the technology and the regulators to the economics and a pre-mortem; it is the record read by someone who did not write it.The decision ledger holds every decision that shapes the venture, each with what was rejected and what it closes off. The dependency map is the record's own graph of decisions, mechanisms, invariants and closed lists, and the architecture map is the same graph drawn for the harness alone. Two further artefacts live in the repository rather than on the site: the surfacing report, SURFACING_REPORT.md, which states the founder's bar item by item and what the harness and the two review passes returned, and the batches under tools/depmap/batches/, one record per batch of changes with the closure the map returned for it.

AI.fred is a personal AI that builds your context, task by task, until it can act in your personal life the way someone who has known you for years would. It handles the ordinary things, a table, a return, a rental search, a trip, and it is built so that it can also be trusted with the ones you dread: the bank, the visa, the claim, the admission. The dreaded tasks are why people come. The ordinary ones are why they stay, and why the context compounds fast enough to be worth anything. AI.fred is a working title. This publication is a design record, not an offer.
