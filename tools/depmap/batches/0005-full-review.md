# Batch 0005: Full Review 11/09

Recorded 2026-09-11, the founder's direction after batch 0004: a comprehensive
review of the idea against the reality of today, written at the level of a
seed partner's memo and published on the workspace as its own section,
`docs/08-review/`. The page is a review of the record, not a revision of it:
it takes no decision and edits no page, so the map's nodes and edges are
unchanged. It is recorded here because it names the changes it asks the record
to make, and those are the founder's to decide, one batch at a time.

## What the review is

Sixteen sections, eighteen figures in the record's diagram language, twelve
tables and 122 sources: the technology as it stands on 11 September 2026, the
technologies on the horizon, the regulatory landscape in India, the United
Kingdom and the United States, the market and the comparables, the economics
the record defers, the team the plan needs, a red-team read of the trust
design, the product and its path to customers, a ranked pre-mortem, what
would need to be true before money, questions for the founder, and a table of
what the record should change. Its verdict is "not yet", with the conditions
under which a narrower company would be funded.

Its evidence carries the record's own chips: `verified` where a source was
read in full at source or a faithful mirror, `to-verify` where it was seen as
an excerpt or could not be reached from the session, and `assumption` where a
number is the review's own estimate with its inputs named. The page status is
`to-verify` by the ordinary precedence.
