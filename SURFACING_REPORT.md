## 4. Every decision taken during the build that shaped the result

- DR-012, the design plan: an engineering dossier with a numbering voice, a title block with stamp and control block, a two-list rail, a sources apparatus, a single diagram canvas and vocabulary, four page kinds and a single-sheet print mode. Taken after an independent three-lens critique (generic-default hunter, accessibility and print reviewer, cold-URL investor) rejected the first draft's documentation-site skeleton.
- DR-013, the verification standard: "verified" in this edition means checked against a search-engine excerpt of the named, dated source, because the build environment blocked page reads and capped web searches at 200 per session. The re-read from source is an open item in 07 with the next editor as owner.
- DR-014, the substitution framing: framing A (boundary-sequence led) chosen over framing B (ledger-led) by two of three independent judges (the third timed out), with eight named grafts from B absorbed into A.
- Page-level status as a function of the page's own inline chips, checked by the harness (recorded in DR-012).
- The stamp and the status chip carried together in every page header, with the same word (objection C in 07).
- The five-word chip vocabulary applied at claim level and page level, with verified never a page status.
- The alias grammar, class table, and the three entry doors for a real value (03, from framing A with grafts).
- The pseudonym issued per task, stable across that task's steps and unlinkable across tasks (04; mirrored in the console).
- L1 never executes an irreversible step; irreversible human steps route to L2 or L3 with the AI as the checking party; when the AI executes an irreversible step an L3 worker holds the human seat (04; 02).
- One-tap confirmations, envelope edits and verdicts land on a surface that authenticates the user whatever channel the conversation runs in (01; 02).
- A family member is a separate delegator whose own one-tap delegation precedes any identity act of theirs (01; open item in 07).
- Refusal of diagnosis, legal advice and investment recommendation as regulated advice (01).
- The pilot's vertical sequence: banking and wealth, identity and security, legal and administrative first; health after telephony has passed its gate; travel and family affairs last (06; amber in 07).
- The lapse transition records the state it left, so measurement can separate abandoned tasks from unjudged ones (02; 07).
- The manifest is built from the pages themselves (title, description, status chip, revised date), so the pages stay the single canonical source.
- Netlify configuration adds an X-Robots-Tag header alongside the per-page no-index directive.
- The decisions ledger and the root index were written by the main context rather than delegated, to conserve the account's usage allowance for the mandatory fresh-context passes.
- The 143 smaller decisions taken by the page writers during the content stage are logged line by line in the build's decisions log (scratchpad passes/build-decisions.md), and the coherence and adversarial fixers' decisions in their group logs; none reopens a section 1 decision.

## 8. Turn count against the cap

The brief's cap is 40 turns.
