## 0. Mission

Two outcomes, in two phases, one run.

Phase A makes this repository cheap for a fresh Claude Code session to work
in, without changing a byte of what the reader receives: every file a session
edits is under 3,000 words of prose, and a `CLAUDE.md` tells a fresh session
the map, the rules and the tools before it opens anything. The reason:
`docs/02-architecture/index.html` is 22,700 words of prose,
`03-trust-and-data` is 24,100, `08-review` is 25,800, each around 50,000
tokens as HTML; a session that opens two has spent its context before its
first edit, and `BUILD_BRIEF.md` is a build-time instruction no session reads
unless told.

Phase B gives the publication a front door and five owner views, so that a
person joining reads what they own and nothing else, without a word of the
design being written twice. Today a newcomer lands on a rail of nine sections,
twenty-seven records and 172,000 words. After this they land on a 1,500-word
summary, then a page naming their sections, decisions and open items, then a
reading path. The principle: views, not copies. Every new page is generated
from `roles.json`, `manifest.json`, `tools/depmap/graph.json` and blocks marked
in the canonical fragments; the only hand-written prose is one capped frame
per generated page. Where a view needs a fact it transcludes the marked block;
it never restates it.

You are the tooling engineer and, in Phase B, the ownership cartographer. You
are not the writer, the designer or the strategist: no prose changes, no design
changes, no decision reopened, no page moved.

## 2. Phase A deliverables (exact)

### 2.1 `src/pages/` and `tools/build.mjs`

- `src/pages/` mirrors `docs/`. For each page listed in `manifest.json` except
  `depmap/index.html` (generated by `depmap.mjs view --site`, never a source):
  - under 3,000 words of prose: `src/pages/<path>` is the page, verbatim;
  - 3,000 words or over: `src/pages/<path minus .html>/` is a directory of
    fragments concatenated in filename order: `000-head.html` (everything
    before the first top-level `<section>` that carries an `<h2>`), one
    `NNN-<h2 id>.html` per such section, `999-foot.html` (everything after the
    last). Cut at line boundaries so every fragment starts and ends on a whole
    line.
- `node tools/build.mjs` writes `docs/` from `src/pages/` by verbatim copy or
  verbatim concatenation. No templating, no transformation, no whitespace
  rewriting. Deterministic. The build carries a list of generated pages
  (empty in Phase A, filled in Phase B) that have no source under `src/pages/`.
- `node tools/build.mjs --check` exits non-zero, printing `file:line message`,
  when any page in `docs/` differs from what a build would write, or when a
  page exists on one side and not the other.
- Produce `src/pages/` with a one-off splitting script, never by hand. Delete
  the script once §3 passes.

### 2.2 `tools/verify.mjs`, section 9

Append a ninth section in the existing comment style that runs the freshness
check and fails in the same `file:line message` form.

### 2.3 DR-028 and the DR-010 revision

- `docs/decisions/dr-028-fragments-and-build.html`, carrying the four sections
  the harness checks (`decision`, `rejected`, `closes-off`, `record-status`),
  copied from a neighbour's template. Decision: pages are assembled from HTML
  fragments by a dependency-free script, and the harness holds the committed
  pages to freshness. Why: DR-010 §1.2 rested on the page count being small
  enough for hand discipline to beat machinery; the pages are now 22,000 to
  26,000 words and a session cannot hold two. Rejected: Markdown canonical
  (DR-010's argument still holds); splitting into more pages (moves two hundred
  loci and changes what a reader receives); leaving it (the cost above). Closes
  off: a page edited in `docs/` directly is overwritten on the next build, and
  the check catches the drift.
- Add DR-028 to `docs/decisions/index.html` and `manifest.json` in the form
  the neighbours use. Revise DR-010 as §1.1 says.
- Add `dr-028` to `tools/depmap/graph.json` per `tools/depmap/SCHEMA.md`: kind,
  facets, loci, and an edge to `dr-010` in the schema's edge vocabulary. Run
  `extract`, then `check`.

### 2.4 `CLAUDE.md`, three of them

Root `CLAUDE.md`, under 120 lines, no heading deeper than `##`, in this order:

1. What the repository is, in three lines.
2. The map: one line per top-level path.
3. The editing rule: edit `src/pages/`, never `docs/`; then
   `node tools/build.mjs`; then `node tools/verify.mjs`.
4. The reading rule: read `manifest.json` summaries first; open only the
   fragment the task needs; never open a whole section page.
5. The dependency rule: before editing a fixed decision, a mechanism stated on
   more than one page, or a closed list, run
   `node tools/depmap.mjs impact <node>` and save the batch under
   `tools/depmap/batches/`.
6. The noise rule: do not open `models/out/`, `tools/depmap/refs.json`,
   `tools/depmap/batches/`, `docs/08-review/` or `SURFACING_REPORT.md` unless
   the task names them.
7. The conventions of `BUILD_BRIEF.md` §4 in five lines.
8. Where the fixed decisions live: `BUILD_BRIEF.md` §1, opened only when a task
   touches one.

`models/CLAUDE.md`, under 30 lines: what each script produces, where outputs
go, how to regenerate. `tools/depmap/CLAUDE.md`, under 15 lines: the command
surface and the change protocol, pointing at `SCHEMA.md`.

### 2.5 `models/out/`

Read the scripts under `models/` and `tools/figures/` to determine which files

### 2.7 `README.md`

Cap: 65 turns, of which Phase A takes no more than 30. Early exits: a step 2
failure; a `depmap.mjs check` failure you cannot trace to your own change; any
need to edit prose; in Phase B, text identity failing for a cause that is not
your own markup, or a generated page that cannot pass a harness section
without changing `tokens.css` or `base.css`. A section you cannot assign
without a judgement the founder should make is assigned to `ceo`, listed, and
not an exit. On an early exit, commit what passes, write whichever report the
phase owes, stop. An exit in Phase B leaves the gate commit intact.

## 7. Forbidden moves

- Editing page prose, anchors, tokens, CSS, JavaScript or figures; in Phase B,
  changing the text of any existing element, including to make a key block
  read better in transclusion.
- Converting anything to Markdown.
- Adding a dependency or a package manager.
- Touching `netlify.toml` or the publish directory.
- Reopening any §1 decision, including the HTML-canonical half of DR-010.
- Hand-writing a list the build can generate.
- Two owners for a section, or a section with none.
- Moving `08-review` or any page.
- A `CLAUDE.md` that restates the brief. It points; it does not repeat.
- A frame over 200 words, or a front door over 1,500.
- Em-dashes anywhere.
- Narrating progress in a report. State results.
