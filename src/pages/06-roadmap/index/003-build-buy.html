3Build versus buy

We buy what must be boring and audited and build what carries the promise: four components bought, sixteen built. Table 6.1 gives the reason for each and what the choice commits us to, since buying is a commitment to someone else's roadmap, regions and outages.

Table 6.1 Buy the layers whose value is being audited and dull; build every layer that touches the promise.
ComponentDecisionReasonWhat it commits us to
Tokenisation vaultbuyHolding secrets and being audited for it is a product; providers of the Skyflow, VGS and Basis Theory class sell regional hosting and attestations to-verify. Recorded as DR-008.Its regions constrain the residency map; its SDK stays inside the substitutor and the hash service, and its edge proxy stands in front of the gateway and the adapters; its outages are ours. The swap interface is the five operations 03 specifies plus the proxy contract; the swap test is re-tokenisation to a second provider behind them, run before the pilot.
TelephonybuyCarrier interconnect and numbers in three countries are not our business. Programmable-telephony platforms expose per-leg muting, keypad tones and synthesised speech to-verify, more than this design needs, because the substitutor places nothing on a call (DR-021).Per-leg dialling under our controller, keypad tones for navigation, per-country numbers and caller identity, and both legs forked to our pipeline. No media server, no server-side mute and no synthesised speech are required of the platform, because no value is placed on a call. Provider-side recording, transcription and voicemail disabled by contract and configuration, so the provider holds no recording and the recordings and transcripts paths of the threat model in 03 rest on our pipeline, never on the carrier, and the worker's-audio path is closed by scope rather than by a mechanism, because no value is on a call; call media forked into our evidence pipeline's memory; per-country numbers and caller identity. The swap test is a second carrier behind the same interface, with the audio canary repeated on it.
Browser sandboxbuyIsolated, streamed browser sessions are a product category to-verify. What matters sits on top and is ours: the driver, the stream that renders dots, the DOM masking.The driver interface is ours: the privileged driver channel, document and accessibility-tree access, the compositor hooks and read-back disabled. Provider-side session recording and screenshots disabled, so the stream is the only representation that leaves the sandbox, as 02 states, and the closure of the worker's screen in the threat model rests on the task card of DR-022 and, where a page is streamed at all, on our masker, not on the sandbox; the substitutor's driver inside its boundary; no session state outside our control. The swap test is a second sandbox behind the same driver, with the browser canary repeated on it.
ModelsbuyFrontier models are rented, not built; the planner and the classifier are prompts, tools, evaluation sets and guard code around a model that must be swappable.Aliases only in every prompt and no vault SDK in the model runtime; no training on our data; regional inference where a legal pack demands it, if offered to-verify. The swap interface is the planner's and the classifier's model call; the swap test is a second model run against the evaluation sets of stage 2.
PlannerbuildThe plan is the product: step vocabulary, re-planning on outcome codes and the envelope proposal are our judgement made executable.An evaluation set per task type, expected plan shapes and envelopes before the pilot and verdict rates joined to it once verdicts exist, that every model swap and prompt change must pass.
Task cardsbuildNeed-to-know is a design property: a pseudonymised client, only the fields the step needs, as aliases.The card generator is the only path to a worker's screen.
Substitution componentbuildThe one component with detokenise rights: small enough to audit line by line, specific to our three surfaces, so nobody sells it.Isolation; a fixed interface; never returns a value; writes the ledger; checks the envelope and the policy layer before every injection.
Authority envelopesbuildThe envelope and the point-of-no-return test are the second fatal's mitigation, enforced in our code at every executor, not configured in someone else's.The envelope check lives in the substitutor, the classifier and the dual-control step; mid-task edits apply as DR-004 states.
State machinebuildThe invariant is architectural; a workflow engine's defaults, implicit terminal states and silent timeouts, are what DR-005 forbids.Explicit transitions, a live timer on every non-terminal state, the watchdog and the exceptions board, under fault-injection tests.
Worker consolebuildThe alias-only rule made visible. A generic service desk would render fields we must never render.Alias-only rendering, outcome codes, the exceptions board, and the invariant text identical to 02.
Access ledgerbuildThe trust story in plain words, written at every placement, view and exposure and read by the user: the customer-facing proof of the promise.Append-only; one line per placement, view or exposure, placement lines written by the substitutor; readable per task from the user's device; aliases only, so the ledger is not a leak path.
Intake gatewaybuildOne door for every channel, producing the request format 02 fixes; it sits behind the vault's edge proxy and holds aliases only.No vault SDK, credential or route; the channel is invisible downstream; a bounce goes to the device binding, never the thread.
Passport adaptersbuildOne interface for every market's rails, the seam of DR-007; a rail's response terminates at the vault's proxy in front of the adapter.Four operations and no others, the fourth being to present a document, an identity attribute or a payment instruction to a third party through the substitution component like any other placement, so that a delegated debit under the cap is a placement under the substitutor's ordered checks and the rail's own binding of a token to the task and its cap is a second layer to-verify, never the closure; adapter conformance per 4.1; a new rail is a schema reviewed with the legal pack.
ClassifierbuildThe point-of-no-return test and the rule of classification, deterministic from the step-type catalogue and the flag table.The evaluation set of stage 2 passed on every change; an unknown step type bounces.
Policy layerbuildThe mechanism is core, keyed by jurisdiction and act type; the table per market is the legal pack's.Three values and a condition line; a missing row evaluates to bounce; the signed table before the pilot opens.
Context storebuildA temporal knowledge graph with provenance on every fact, DR-016; no library is chosen, and the five tests of that record choose one at stage 3.Alias-only sensitive fields; point-in-time queries; no vault SDK in its runtime.
Task servicebuildThe host of the state machine: the step runner, the three surface controllers, the card generator, the timers and the watchdog.Its three controllers are the only writers of a placement request; the signed step context; the client-number mapping of 04 lives here and nowhere else.
Evidence pipelinebuildCapture, redact, verify, store, inside the real-value zone, with the verifier built by a different team: the leak path DR-009 names.No raw artefact persisted; the store accepts only a verifier-signed artefact; the canary test on every surface.
Hash servicebuildThe one vault-facing interface of ours outside the substitutor: the keys held here, two digest sets issued, candidate windows digested on request.No detokenise right; rate limits per frame and segment; a breach is an incident.
Device surfacebuildThe user's authenticated app or web session: envelope confirmation, extension approval, bounces, the outcome report, the verdict and the ledger view.Authenticated whatever the channel; a tap on a chat thread authorises nothing; the universal bounce-back needs no adapter.

Every bought component has a swap test before the pilot opens, because a bought component we cannot leave is a built component with worse terms: its row names the interface it stands behind, and the test is a second supplier behind that interface passing the same gate. Nothing built sees a bought component's raw output without the vault's edge proxy or a masker of ours between, and none of the four is a trusted box. The vault provider is a named path in the threat model in 03, closed by contract, one detokenise principal and log reconciliation; the carrier and the sandbox are paths 17 and 18 of the same model: the sandbox is closed by the structured card and by server-side masking before any stream reaches it, and the carrier carries audio that holds no vaulted value at all, with provider-side recording and session capture disabled by contract and configuration so that no provider copy exists for the path to run through.
