2Objections formed against fixed decisions

The brief fixes its decisions and asks that an objection be recorded and complied with, not argued. Four were formed during the build.

A. Full delegation including identity acts as the ceiling (brief 1.2; DR-003)
The ceiling is right as a design stance. The objection is about the lobbying agenda's length: in the UK and the US an operator stating a customer's identity number or answering security questions to an institution may be treated by that institution as impersonation regardless of the customer's consent, and the institution's own terms may forbid it. The policy layer handles this at runtime as a flag, so nothing in the design changes. Complied with: the design carries the ceiling, and Table 3.5 in 03 records it, delegated in the identity-act cells it can be, undergoing identification and signing reading bounce in every market and every cell to-verify; the pilot's runtime setting for identity acts in the UK and the US is bounce until counsel's answer sets the flag; the difference between the two tables is the item above, not a change to the ceiling.
B. The amber token as given (brief 3)
The amber colour the brief specifies does not reach AA contrast as text on the second ground. Complied with by never using amber as a text colour: it appears only as a dot, a stroke or a stamp outline. Overtaken on 2026-09-10 by the dark ground of DR-012, revised: the amber token now reads 9.6:1 on paper and 8.8:1 on the second ground, so it would pass as text; the rule that amber is never text stands, because it is a status colour and not a voice.
C. A document status chip in the page header (brief 9.2)
The design critique found that a stamp, not a chip, is the dossier's device for a document's status. Complied with by carrying both, with the same word on each, and having the harness check that they agree.
D. The interim access mitigation (brief 1.11)
An unguessable URL plus a no-index directive is not access control, for the reasons in 1.3. Complied with: the mitigation is in place and the item is open above with the founder as owner.
