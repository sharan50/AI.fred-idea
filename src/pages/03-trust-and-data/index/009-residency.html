9The data-residency map

Sensitive values stay in the market's region and only aliases travel to the floor. That is the whole map, and it is why the vault is bought: a provider's regional hosting gives each market a vault instance in its own region, and everything of ours that holds a value transiently, the substitutor, the surfaces and the evidence pipeline, is deployed in that region too. The floor in Bengaluru, running 24/7 under the shift design in 04, receives the rendered stream, the task cards, the ledger writes and the masked artefacts a review needs: dots, aliases and masked material, never a value. Figure 3.4 draws the three markets, and the accent is the one crossing that needs contractual safeguards: personal data of UK and EU residents, even in alias form and pseudonymised, processed by staff in Bengaluru is a restricted transfer under UK GDPR that needs a transfer mechanism (the international data transfer agreement or addendum) and a transfer risk assessment, with the processor terms to match to-verify. The US crossing is governed by contract and by the applicable state law rather than by a transfer regime, which the legal pack confirms per cohort to-verify. The India crossing is domestic. Model inference for a market runs in that market's region where the model vendor offers it, otherwise in India under the same safeguards as the floor, and for the pilot that choice follows the first market; in either case it sees aliases to-verify. A region is never overridden for availability: a vault or substitutor outage in one region delays a step; it never routes a value through another region (Table 3.2). Routing is by one key: the market the gateway sets on the request from the user record selects the region, India, the UK or the EU for a UK user, or the US, and that region names the vault instance, the substitutor deployment, the surfaces, the evidence pipeline and the hash service the task will use for its whole life; a step never chooses a region. The rule in DR-007 follows: the user's market fixes where values rest and are placed and the institution's market fixes only the rail, so a UK user acting on an Indian institution keeps their vault, substitutor and surfaces in the UK region, the UK-region browser or line reaches the Indian institution as it reaches any other party, and the India adapter's proxy tokenises the rail's response into the user's UK vault instance. Each market's entry in the map lists, per component, the region and the safeguard, and ships in the legal pack (02); the expansion playbook in 06 adds a row per market.
