10The privacy-law skeleton

The design answers most of what each privacy law asks before the law is opened: minimisation is the alias rule, transparency is the ledger, accuracy and correction are the context store's provenance, transfer rules are the residency map, and consent is a tap on the user's own device. Table 3.7 maps the three regimes to the design at skeleton level so the legal pack knows where to start; it is not the legal pack.
