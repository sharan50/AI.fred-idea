2The idea in one page, at its strongest

At its strongest the idea is this. Every household has a short list of tasks it dreads, and they share a shape: they touch a bank, a government office, an insurer, a hospital or a court; they need the person's own identity numbers, documents and money; they take hours of waiting, calling and re-submitting; and a mistake costs something real. Today those tasks are done badly by the person, or handed to a human assistant who is trusted with everything, or refused outright by the software agents the labs shipped in 2025 and withdrew in 2026. The record's thesis is that the dreaded tasks are the door, that a company which can be trusted with them earns the right to the ordinary ones, and that the context built task by task is what makes the ordinary ones cheap (DR-019, revised 2026-09-13).

The mechanism is what no rival has. The user's real values, an Aadhaar or National Insurance number, a card, a balance, a diagnosis, enter a bought vault at the edge and are replaced by typed aliases before anything on the company's side reads them (03, 1, revised 2026-09-13). The AI plans; a worker on a floor in Bengaluru executes one bounded step at a time from a task card that names the client as a number and every value as an alias (Worker console, revised 2026-09-13); a single component, the substitutor, places a real value into a browser field or a mail body under a signed step context, and writes a ledger line the user can read (03, 3). Every task carries an authority envelope; every step meets a point-of-no-return test; anything irreversible either bounces to the user's own device or runs under dual control (DR-004). OTPs, PINs, bank logins and biometrics never enter the system at all: they are not aliases, they are bounces. The claim the record makes for this design is exact and defensible: "no worker ever sees a real value" is a property of a component boundary, not of a policy, and the specification now tests it.

The economics are stated as one ratio, and the record is right that it is the one. "Delivery cost scales with usage, not with users. The governing number is therefore human minutes per user per month" (05, 2, revised 2026-09-14). Four levers move it: a task allowance of 26 a month, a price of $52 in India, an automation ceiling of 80 per cent and an assisted floor of 1.7 minutes a task; with those set the planning line falls from 246 minutes a user in month 1 to 104 at month 18, 61 at month 36 and 42 at month 60, and the company crosses into contribution around month 25 [EST]. The record does not claim those numbers are true. It claims they are the right numbers to measure, and it built a thirty-task protocol to measure them (Measurement protocol). Figure 8.19 draws the ratio over the plan's own months; the seed window ends where the line is still at 104.

Why now is argued in three parts and two of them hold. The labs shipped consumer agents and withdrew them: Operator closed on 31 August 2025, the agent mode of ChatGPT was removed in August 2026, Atlas closed on 9 August 2026, Google's Mariner ended on 4 May 2026 to-verify [14] to-verify [15], and the survivors hand the keyboard back at a login or a purchase and tell users to keep them off financial, legal and medical sites to-verify [16]. The rails for delegated consent are being built by the state and the networks rather than by the assistants: DigiLocker's requester model already puts the consent screen on the citizen's own device with a stated purpose and an expiry verified [17]; UPI Circle admits software profiles under a cap to-verify [18]; the Treasury is consulting on agentic payments to-verify [11]. The third part, that people are being taught to delegate by the assistants themselves, is a hope about habit, and the record's own endgame page admits the previous cadence of "three or four hard tasks a year" could not form one (DR-019).

The founder's advantage is the record itself. Nothing in the Indian field publishes what its assistants may see; nothing in the American field masks values from its own staff; the labs will not touch the wedge. Twelve pages, twenty-seven decisions with rejected alternatives, a dependency map, a model with its critique attached, a threat model with twenty-six insider paths, and an executable specification that fails when a page changes a word of an invariant: that is more design discipline than most seed-stage companies show at their A, and it was produced by one person in about six weeks. The bet at its strongest is that the same discipline, applied to a cohort of two hundred Indian households and a floor of six, turns the ratio page from a prior into a measurement before the money runs out, and that the measurement lands near the planning line. If it does, the company that results is the only one in three markets that can be trusted with the tasks that matter, at a price its rivals cannot match because their human share never fell.
