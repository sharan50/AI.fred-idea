5Technologies on the horizon, and who owns their distribution

The review of 11 September drew the horizon as the labs retreating and the platforms arriving. Both moved in a week, and the second faster than the first. This section lists what is arriving by mechanism, who ships it, when it becomes a commodity, and what it does to the record, and it includes the platform owners the record's landscape table treats as a separate column, because the distribution they own is the threat and not their models.

5.1Consumer agents from the platform owners

Meta launched a consumer agent in the United States on 8 September 2026 that sends mail, fills forms, books travel, buys and negotiates, added phone calls on 17 September, and will distribute through WhatsApp, which is the channel the record's Indian users already live in to-verify [42]. OpenAI removed the agent mode of ChatGPT in August, replaced it with a work product that pauses for confirmation, and on 25 August gave its cloud browser persistent logins with an optional "auto approve" per site to-verify [14]. Claude in Chrome has been generally available on every paid plan since 26 August, acting without per-action approval behind a safety classifier, with a help page that tells users to keep it off financial, legal and medical sites to-verify [16]. Google ended Mariner, put auto-browse in Chrome for paid tiers at 20 and 200 actions a day, shipped Gemini Spark as a background agent over mail, calendar and drive, and published the second version of its agent-payments protocol with "human not present" flows on 28 April 2026 to-verify [15] verified [43]. Apple's Siri AI shipped with iOS 27 on 14 September as an opt-in, English-only beta to-verify [44]. Amazon put Alexa+ into India on 16 September 2026 in English, Hindi and Hinglish, free with Prime and Rs 2,000 a month otherwise, with bookings through Swiggy, MakeMyTrip and others to-verify [45]; Jio's call agent joins calls on a wake word and, with consent, books cabs, food and tables and does self-KYC for an eSIM to-verify [46].

What every one of these has in common is the record's thesis: at a login, a payment or an identity act, the keyboard goes back to the user, and the vendor says so. What they also have in common is that they are free or bundled, on the device the user already holds, from the company that already has the user's mail. The record's everyday endgame, "the ordinary things, a table, a return, a rental search, a trip", is exactly the list Alexa+ and Muse now do in India and the United States for nothing. The endgame was promoted to day one on 13 September; on the same day its price went to zero for the incumbents. The consequential wedge is untouched, and it is the only thing that is.

5.2Payment rails for agents

Visa's Trusted Agent Protocol signs an agent's requests so a merchant can tell an authorised agent from a scraper; the protocol carries account references, not credentials, and its repository says nothing on liability verified [47]. It is reported live in Europe with more than thirty issuers from 2 July 2026 and inside ChatGPT with caps and approvals from June to-verify [48]; Mastercard's Agent Pay is reported live in Australia, New Zealand, Singapore and India by March 2026 to-verify [49]. In India, UPI Circle's software profiles are a closed-group pilot under a Rs 15,000 monthly cap and Rs 5,000 a transaction, merchant payments only, with the user's explicit action on each debit, onboarded by a payment-service-provider bank after an app security evaluation to-verify [18]; the Unified Agent Protocol the record dates to the festival is "in development" with no timeline to-verify [6], and the NPCI chairman said on the festival's stage that AI should not be what approves a payment to-verify [5]. In the United Kingdom, commercial variable recurring payments went live on 2 June 2026 for utilities, government and charities, with e-commerce in the second half to-verify [50], and the Treasury's consultation on agentic payments closes on 6 October with no date for a rule to-verify [11].

For the record this is good and bad in equal measure. Good: every rail is converging on the shape the design already has, a user-held consent with a cap, a per-task token, an agent that is registered rather than trusted; the design will not need to change to use them. Bad: registration is by the networks and the banks, to their partners, and a consumer agent company gets a token only by contract with an issuer, a wallet or a token service; none of these is a licence, and none is open to a seed-stage company in Bengaluru before it has a cohort. The commoditisation date for delegated payment is set by the platform owners and it is inside the seed window. Table 8.16 gives each item its date.

5.3Well-funded rivals in the user's own accounts

Instinct raised $250m at $2.5bn in 2026 for an agent that lives in the user's mail, was reported to retain mail in plain text, and on 17 September 2026 launched a concierge tier for phone calls and high-touch bookings whose staffing is not published to-verify [51]; Hark raised $700m at $6bn before shipping a product to-verify [51]. These are the record's "see everything" column with a hundred times its capital. If Instinct's concierge is human-staffed, the record has a Californian rival doing its hybrid at Californian wages, and the record's wage arbitrage is its only cost defence against it. The record should watch one thing: the day one of these publishes a masking policy for its own staff, the trust design stops being unique and starts being table stakes.
