1Executive verdict

VerdictNot yet. The record now knows exactly what it has to measure and has measured none of it, and the round it asks for is sized to a line its own geography decision has already left.

DecisionNot yet, and the offer is closer than it was on 11 September. We would fund the India-only company that review described, at a seed sized net of tax to the record's own conservative line, on four conditions met before the cheque rather than after it: a signed technical co-founder who has shipped a tokenisation or payments boundary; the thirty-task run of the record's own measurement protocol, with its numbers; two hundred refundable deposits against the $52 price; and Indian counsel's signature on the identity rows of Table 3.5.

What would prove this wrongIf by 31 December 2026 the measured run returns an AI-only share above 40 per cent and a median assisted time under twelve minutes on the record's own rubric, and two hundred households have paid a deposit, the verdict is wrong and we would say so and fund. It is wrong the other way if counsel returns the identity rows as bounce: then the wedge is admin, the price is Faff's, and no round is the right round.

This is a review of the AI.fred record in its edition of 15 September 2026, the last commit before this page (7f2f6d9, merged on 17 September as fa2aaa8), whose pages carry revision dates of 13, 14 and 15 September. It is the second partner review the record carries. The first is dated 11 September and sits beside this one (Full Review 11/09); the founder's revision of 13 September answered it, and section 3 says which of its findings were answered in substance, which in wording, and which stand. Everything below was produced with the same tools and under the same limits as that review, and section 18 reports them: the search budget ran out mid-review and nearly every regulator, vendor and press host was blocked, so primary texts were read where a dated mirror exists and marked to-verify where only an excerpt could be seen.

Three findings carry the verdict. First, the round does not reconcile with the sequence. Table 5.4 asks for $2.5m to $3m, and the same page's own arithmetic says that on "the foreign-led sequence of DR-023, compared without its commerce layer, the same arithmetic asks for 22.3 crore through month 18 and 22.3 crore again after it", so that the seed band's "floor is exactly equal to it" (05, 7, revised 2026-09-14). That is before the consumption tax neither model carries, which the record itself puts at about a seventh of Indian revenue and a sixth of British. Read the two sentences together and the seed is one number short of the record's own conservative arithmetic on the sequence it commits to, with no buffer, gross of tax. Section 3 draws it.

Second, every number that decides the company is still a prior, and the record says so on every page: the launch automation share, the minutes a task takes, the churn, the cost of a paid arrival. What the outside evidence adds is that the two hybrids that ever published an automation share sat at or below the record's launch prior and nowhere near its ceiling: Facebook M served at most about 30 per cent of its answers from the machine after two years and closed to-verify [1]; Google Duplex, on one narrow task with the best model of its year, still started about a quarter of calls with a person and needed one in a further 15 per cent to-verify [2]. The record's planning line assumes 42 per cent AI-only at launch and 80 at the ceiling, and its cash trough, its month-36 minutes and its lifetime value all follow from that slope. Section 9 sets the prior beside the measured comparables; it is the single figure the thirty-task run has to move first.

Third, the company has one hire it cannot substitute and has not made. The trust design is the whole premium over Faff and Hulp, and it is now reduced to reference code by one person, the founder, whose own specification records thirty-one places where a second engineer would have decided otherwise. The open-items page lists the vault vendor before the founding engineer who would choose it (07, revised 2026-09-14). Section 11 says what the hire is and why it is a condition rather than a plan.

What changed since 11 September is real and not cosmetic, and it should be said before the rest, because a founder who takes a hostile review point by point and rewrites the record in four days is the kind of founder this business needs. Economics that were deferred are modelled: twenty thousand paths at a fixed seed with a rationale and a critique beside them (The model, revised 2026-09-14). Telephony masking, the least credible mechanism of the previous edition, is dropped and the call is admitted to be a person speaking in the ordinary way (DR-021). Pixel masking became structured task cards (DR-022); the attested floor is staged (DR-006); pricing is metered (DR-024); acquisition has a mechanism and a budget (DR-025); and the record was then compiled into an executable specification with 101 tests. Those changes also expose what they cannot fix. A model of priors is still priors. A specification proves that the pages are consistent, not that the system is safe. And the geography decision moved the cash need the wrong way while the round stayed where it was.

What we would do is in the decision above and is the substance of sections 15 and 16. The narrower company is the one the review of 11 September described and this edition half-built: India only inside the seed, a browser-and-draft pilot, a day-shift floor in one room, which the record now says is the pilot anyway (04, 2, revised 2026-09-13), a co-founder before the cheque, and the thirty tasks measured before the harness is extended past the vault.
