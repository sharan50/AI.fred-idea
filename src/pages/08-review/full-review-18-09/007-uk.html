7Regulatory position: the United Kingdom

The record says of the United Kingdom that "the research for this edition read none of it at source, so every UK claim is to-verify" (03, 5.3, revised 2026-09-13). This review read one text at source, the Payment Services Regulations, and everything else in excerpt, so the standard is the record's own. The finding is nonetheless firm, because the excerpts agree: every act the wedge needs in Britain is a licence, a named-individual mandate or a bounce, and the two rails that would change that have no date.

7.1Data: a fiduciary in Bengaluru with a British representative

The Data (Use and Access) Act received Royal Assent on 19 June 2025 and its main commencement was on 5 February 2026; the Information Commissioner's Office becomes the Information Commission on 30 September 2026 to-verify [69]. There is no adequacy decision for India, so every transfer of a British user's data to the Bengaluru floor needs the international data transfer agreement or the addendum plus a transfer risk assessment, on guidance updated on 15 January 2026 to-verify [70], and a company with no British establishment needs an Article 27 representative, since the exemption is unavailable to continuous health-adjacent processing to-verify [70]. Class: build plus contract; none of it is in the record's residency map, which draws the vault region and not the transfer instrument.

7.2Identity: no delegation route exists

The digital identity trust framework reached version 1.0 on 3 March 2026 with an in-force date reported as 1 September 2026 and not confirmed; a relying party needs no certification to-verify [12]. GOV.UK One Login has no route for one person to act for another; the service is reported to be "exploring acting on behalf" to-verify [12]. Banks accept third-party mandates for named individuals, some limited to twelve months and branch-only with no online or card access, and otherwise want a power of attorney to-verify [71]; the NHS gives a proxy their own login, with a national proxy service at 68 practices in pilot to-verify [72]. Money-laundering regulation 39 lets a bank rely only on a regulated person, so a form a delegate completed creates no reliance, and regulation 28(10) obliges the bank to verify anyone "purporting to act" for a customer to-verify [73]. So the "present a document", "state an identity number" and "complete a KYC form" rows read, in Britain, "bounce, or the user names the company's worker as their mandated individual, per institution, in a branch". The record's pilot table already bounces every identity act outside India (06, 2.3, revised 2026-09-14); this section says the table is not conservative but correct, for the whole seed window.

7.3Payments and accounts: a licence or an agent

Account information and payment initiation are regulated activities under the Payment Services Regulations 2017, regulation 2(1); a firm carries them on as an authorised or registered provider, as an agent of one under regulation 34, or as a customer of a regulated provider's API; a British body corporate authorised under the regulations must have its head office in the United Kingdom, regulation 6(4) verified [10]. Commercial variable recurring payments went live on 2 June 2026 for utilities, government and charities, e-commerce in the second half to-verify [50]; the Mills review of 6 July 2026 made seven recommendations and no rules to-verify [74]; the Treasury's consultation on agentic payments, opened on 14 July 2026, closes on 6 October with questions on consent, authentication and liability and no date for anything to-verify [11]. Class: contract (a regulated provider's API for account data, with the token in the vault) or bounce; nothing an agent could hold appears before 2028 [EST].

7.4The tasks themselves: two of the wedge's verticals are licensed

Helping with an insurance claim is a regulated claims-management activity: articles 89G onward of the Regulated Activities Order cover seeking out, advising on, investigating and representing a claim about a financial product, so "an insurance claim and its first follow-up" is a licence or a refusal, with a thin clerical line to-verify [75]. Immigration advice or services in the course of business without qualification is an offence under section 84 of the Immigration and Asylum Act 1999, policed by the Immigration Advice Authority since 16 January 2025 to-verify [76]; the record's "visa application pack" is refuse or licence in Britain, and only foreign visas may sit outside, which is to-verify. Fraud Act section 2 needs a false representation with dishonesty: "I act for Mrs X" is not one, "I am Mrs X" is to-verify [77]. Calls from abroad presenting a British number have been blocked by Ofcom's general condition C6 since 15 May 2023, so the floor's calls to British institutions must originate on a British carrier to-verify [35]. The Competition and Markets Authority's guidance of 9 March 2026 holds a business responsible for its agents and says nothing of consumer-side agents; the consumer-protection powers under the 2024 Act carry fines to 10 per cent of turnover to-verify [78]. Class: refusal for claims and visas absent a licence, build for the rest.
