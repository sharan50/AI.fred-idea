16Questions for the founder

The questions we would ask in the room, in the order we would ask them. Several test whether the founder holds the design without the document; the rest test the decisions this review says should be reversed.

  1. Seam 10 of your own specification says the hash service holds every vault value in memory to compute the digest sets. Walk us through why that is not a second vault, or tell us which vendor will compute the digests for you and what you asked them.
  2. DR-023 opens the United Kingdom between months 8 and 14. On the day the entity is incorporated, how many Indian households will have paid the invoice price for six months, what will the AI-only share be on their tasks, and what number would make you not incorporate?
  3. Your Table 5.4 seed is $2.5m to $3m and your own section 7 says the sequence of record asks for $2.5m through month 18 and $2.5m again after it. Which is the raise, and what is cut if it is the first?
  4. What is the price on the waiting list, and does the customer pay GST on top of it or inside it? Who told you?
  5. Name the last five people you spoke to about this who are not the model: a household that lost a task to a lapsed deadline, an Indian bank or hospital that would let a data-centre browser fill its form, a vault vendor, a carrier and a lawyer. What did each one change in the record?
  6. Your thirty-task protocol runs on your own accounts and its own text says the bounce threshold cannot trip by construction. When you run it, what is the number you will report for the bounce rate, and how will an investor know it is not the number you chose?
  7. What is your position at Faff today, what did you assign to them, when did the six verticals and the three tiers you carried in from prior work exist, and who else at Faff has seen this record?
  8. A desk at Max Healthcare asks your worker for the patient's date of birth on a mapping call. Say the next sentence the worker says, then tell us what the step closes as, where the task goes, and how many of the pilot's health tasks end there.
  9. Meta shipped a consumer agent on 8 September that books, buys and calls, on WhatsApp, at zero. Which everyday task will a Bengaluru household do with you instead, next month, and why?
  10. Your AI-only prior at launch is 42 per cent. Every hybrid that published a number stalled below 30. What is different about your first hundred tasks, in mechanism rather than in model?
  11. Which technical co-founder has read the thirty-one seams, and which three did they decide differently from the code?
  12. A user narrows the envelope while a committing placement is inside its window and the value has not yet left. Your pages say the placement completes; your open item says the marker is not yet written. Which is it, and what does the ledger say to the user?
  13. If the India identity rows come back from counsel as bounce, is this still the company you are raising for, and what is it instead?
  14. What would make you ship one task type end to end on a bought vault, a bought browser and a ledger, to fifty Indian households, in ninety days, and skip everything else in the roadmap until it is measured?
