14Pre-mortem: it is dead in three years, ranked causes

It is September 2029 and AI.fred is dead. The causes below are ranked by the product of likelihood and impact inside the seed's runway, each with a likelihood and an impact we are willing to state and a kill criterion that can fail. Four of the last review's twelve have moved: the human-share risk is unchanged but better instrumented; the harness-before-customer risk has been joined by a market-before-customer risk; the media-engineer risk is gone with the media path; and the tax and step-cost findings add a new cause that was invisible a week ago. Figure 8.25 places them.
