18The evidence ledger: what was read, how, and what could not be

This review was produced under the same limits as the review of 11 September and reports them in the same way, because a reader who cannot see the limits cannot weigh the findings. The search tool's session budget of two hundred queries ran out part-way through the research, shared across this page and the research it delegated, so the market-sizing, income-distribution and labour-cost queries were never run and their rows above say so. The outbound proxy blocked nearly every regulator, vendor and press host: rbi.org.in, npci.org.in, meity.gov.in, uidai.gov.in, legislation.gov.uk, ico.org.uk, gov.uk, ecfr.gov, consumerfinance.gov, fcc.gov, ftc.gov, the vendors' documentation and every newspaper. What could be read at source was read through GitHub, where dated mirrors of the Code of Federal Regulations, the Indian statutes and rules, the Payment Services Regulations and several vendors' SDKs exist, and each such reading says so in the source list. Everything else is an excerpt: a search-engine result or a quotation in a secondary note, which is the standard the record itself adopted in DR-013 and which this page does not pretend to exceed.

18.1Wording against substance

The review of 11 September records two findings that turned on wording the founder did not intend, the endgame and the everyday scope, and the revision fixed both. This review distinguishes its own findings the same way, so that the founder can fix a sentence where a sentence is the problem and not mistake the rest for sentences. Wording: "nobody on our side ever sees a real value" on the promise page, where the design delivers "no worker" and the specification records the hash service as the exception; "speak to an institution as the customer" in Table 3.5, where every statute read turns on exactly that word and the design means "on behalf of"; and the pilot's identity table framed as caution when it is the rule. Substance: Table 5.4 against DR-023; the Regulation E claim; the Unified Agent Protocol dated to a festival that did not launch it; the vault "bought" in a shape no vendor sells; the automation prior above every measured hybrid; and the three conditions of 11 September that stand. Section 17 assigns each to a page.

18.2What this page is and is not

It is dated 18 September 2026 and will not be edited after that date; if the record answers it, the answer belongs in a dated note above it, as the record did for the review of 11 September. It is hostile to the document and not to the person: every finding names a page and a sentence, and every condition names a test that can fail. It is not legal advice, in any of the three jurisdictions, and it says in every regulatory table which texts were read and which were not. And it is not a substitute for the two things it keeps asking for, a cohort and a lawyer, because no amount of reading produces either.
