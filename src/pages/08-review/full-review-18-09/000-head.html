For the founder and investors

The ideaA subscription personal assistant, AI-planned and human-executed, for the tasks in a household's life that touch money, identity, institutions and health, built so that no worker ever sees a real value and nothing irreversible happens without the user's own tap. The record calls it human-in-the-gap (00, revised 2026-09-13).

Target marketsIndia as delivery base, workflow laboratory and first revenue market; the United Kingdom opened between months 8 and 14; the United States around month 36, each behind a user gate of 400 and 600 (DR-023, revised 2026-09-14).

What exists todayA design record of twelve pages and twenty-seven decision records, a dependency map, a Monte Carlo model of twenty thousand paths, a thirty-task measurement protocol and an executable specification with 101 passing tests. No customer, no cohort, no code that serves a user, no second founder (07, revised 2026-09-14).

The roundSeed, now, $2.5m to $3m for eighteen to twenty-four months of runway, to buy measured unit economics and a first cohort that renews; Series A of $6m to $10m at months 18 to 22 (05, Table 5.4, revised 2026-09-14).

HorizonEighteen months, to March 2028: the seed window as the record sizes it.

Edition reviewedThe record at commit 7f2f6d9 of 15 September 2026, merged as fa2aaa8 on 17 September, whose pages carry revision dates of 13, 14 and 15 September.
