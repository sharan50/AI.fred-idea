6The regulatory landscape: the United Kingdom

The UK is the market where the record's thesis is most in step with the regulators and least in step with the rails. On the thesis side the record is right and, if anything, understates its luck: the FCA's Mills Review of 6 July 2026 names identity, mandates, authority to act, liability and audit as the building blocks of agentic finance and recommends a trusted framework for authorising, identifying and holding agents accountable to-verify [55]; HM Treasury's payments consultation of 14 July 2026 asks in terms whether consent, authentication and unauthorised-transaction liability need rewriting for agent-initiated payments to-verify [56]; the CMA's guidance of 9 March 2026 makes a business answerable for its agent as for an employee to-verify [57]; and the Data (Use and Access) Act loosened automated decision-making from 5 February 2026 to-verify [58]. The record's lobbying agenda in Table 3.5 is, almost line for line, the regulators' own work programme. What the record misses is the timetable: the new payments regime is aimed at the end of 2028, the long-term open finance framework is a 2027 discussion, and the Consumer Duty scope consultation closes on 18 September 2026 with rules in the first quarter of 2027 to-verify [59]. Nothing the record needs from a regulator arrives inside a seed round's runway.

On the rails the record is out of date or structurally mistaken in three places. First, the UK answer to the record's own open question in 07, 1.1 is available today and is not the one the design hopes for: under the Payment Services Regulations 2017 a party that accesses account data or initiates payments for a consumer must be an authorised or registered provider or a registered agent of one; regulation 34 forbids providing the service through an agent unless the agent is on the FCA register, the principal applies for that registration and carries the liability and the professional indemnity insurance, and an authorised institution must be a UK body corporate with its head office in the UK verified [60] to-verify [61]. Reading through a regulated provider under the user's own authentication is the agent model, which means FCA registration, not a design choice. Second, commercial variable recurring payments went live on 2 June 2026 under the UK Payments Initiative, not in the first quarter under Open Banking Limited as Table 3.4 says, and it is a biller-collect rail in which the payee's provider initiates under the consumer's standing consent within limits to-verify [62]; sweeping is me-to-me only. The UK condition on the make-a-payment row does not describe how the rail works. Third, the formal routes the record names for acting on someone's account (third-party mandates, lasting powers of attorney, NHS proxy) all presuppose a named natural person verified in branch or at the surgery, and HMRC now requires anyone who interacts with it on a client's behalf to register as a tax adviser from windows opening 18 May 2026 and no longer accepts a paper 64-8 for limited authorisation to-verify [63] to-verify [64]. The basis row, speak to an institution as the customer, has no UK footing outside these person-shaped routes.

The privacy skeleton in Table 3.7 is right in outline and thin where it matters. It correctly treats Bengaluru processing of UK personal data, even in alias form, as a restricted transfer needing the international data transfer agreement or the addendum and a transfer risk assessment; India has no UK adequacy and is only a longer-term priority for a partnership to-verify [65]. It does not notice that the new Articles 22A to 22D keep the restriction on solely automated significant decisions where special-category data is involved, which is the health vertical, nor that the ICO plans agentic-AI guidance and a statutory automated-decision code to-verify [58]. Telephony from the company's own numbers is consistent with Ofcom's calling-line guidance in force since 29 January 2025; the ICO treats AI voice as an automated calling system, which matters only if the company ever markets by voice, and PECR fines are now at UK GDPR scale to-verify [66]. The digital-identity rails are mid-rollout: version 1.0 of the trust framework took effect at the start of September 2026 with at least fifteen months for providers to uplift, and the wallet is not yet a route a delegate can use to-verify [67].

The item that most changes the plan is the FCA's perimeter. The perimeter report of 26 March 2026 and the Mills Review both flag unregulated AI giving recommendations or intermediation functionally equivalent to regulated activities as an emerging harm, and Mills' first recommendation is to secure and adapt the perimeter to-verify [59] to-verify [55]. The wedge (present a document, fill a form, book, cancel, chase) can be built as an unregulated service with a registered-agent relationship for account data; the endgame of DR-019 (first port of call for everything personal, banking and wealth included) walks into advising, arranging and financial promotion, where an unauthorised firm cannot operate. The record never mentions section 21 of the Financial Services and Markets Act, credit broking or the perimeter. The founder should assume that a UK commercial launch needs either FCA registration as an agent of an account-information or payment-initiation provider at minimum, or a design that keeps every financial step on the user's own device, and should say which.
