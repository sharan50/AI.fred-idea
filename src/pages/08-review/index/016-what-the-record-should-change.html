16What the record should change

This review asks the record to change in the places listed below. Each is a change a partner would expect to see before a second meeting, and each is written so that it can land as a decision record with its rejected alternatives and its closes-off list, or as a revision to a page with its impact run through the dependency map, in the way DR-017 prescribes. Three of them reverse or narrow decisions the record calls fixed (DR-006, DR-015, DR-019); the founder should treat those as this review's recommendation, not as a finding, and decide them.
