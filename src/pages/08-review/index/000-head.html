The trust design is ahead of the field and the wedge is real, but the plan spends a seed on a harness before a customer, the promise can be tested in one market only, and the economics that decide the company are deferred past the round they must justify. This page is an independent review of the record as it stood on 11 September 2026, written at the level of a seed partner's investment memo: what the technology can do today, what is coming that could threaten the idea, the regulatory position in the three target markets, the market, the economics the record defers, the team the plan needs, a red-team read of the trust design, the product and its path to customers, a pre-mortem, and what would need to be true before money.
