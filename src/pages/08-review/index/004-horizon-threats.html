The record's competitive argument rests on two claims about the labs and the platforms: that they have models but no hands (no telephony, no counter, no human for the step that needs one, 00, 5.2) and that 2026 was the year they found the ceiling and withdrew consumer agents (00, 2.1). Read against the 2025 to 2026 sources, the first claim is out of date and the second misreads consolidation as retreat. Google now runs the browser (Gemini in Chrome with auto browse for all US Android users from 18 August 2026), the phone line (agentic calling on Duplex, rolling to everyone in the US this summer and identifying itself as AI from Google), the mailbox (Gmail) and a 24-hour background agent with its own email address (Gemini Spark, announced 19 May 2026) to-verify [22]. The vendors' advice against financial, legal and medical sites that the record leans on in Table 0.1 is a policy line in a release note, not a structural ceiling, and it can be reversed in one release by the companies AI.fred buys its models from.

Two moves cut in opposite directions and the record is silent on both. On 4 August 2026 the Ninth Circuit vacated Amazon's injunction against Perplexity's Comet, holding that an AI agent is a tool, not a person, and that it is the user who accesses the site to-verify [32]; that removes the computer-misuse barrier to agents acting inside a user's own accounts in the US and undercuts the record's premise that acting inside the user's accounts is the weak column. Against that, Cloudflare's default block of the Agent category on ad-bearing pages for new domains from 15 September 2026, Web Bot Auth, and banks' passkeys, device binding and terms that forbid automation let institutions admit only agents they recognise verified [20] verified [21]. Enterprise identity is moving the same way: Okta's agent identity products are generally available and the IETF drafts on acting on behalf of a user are advancing, but all of it is enterprise-first and no consumer bank or government implementation exists to-verify [33].

The deepest threat is to DR-019 rather than to the wedge. The endgame says the context store is the asset and the first port of call is a result of holding it. Apple, Google and Microsoft now hold that context at zero marginal cost, on the user's own device or in their own mailbox, with platform-grade privacy claims; and the institutions are removing the dread from the dreaded tasks by making them first-party: the NHS App's AI triage lets more than a million patients book directly and is due for every NHS App user by April 2028 to-verify [34], GOV.UK Chat launched in the app on 14 May 2026 with agentic task completion stated as the next step to-verify [35], UIDAI's new Aadhaar app with selective sharing passed 21 million downloads in three months, and the banks are piloting their own agents. Meanwhile capital is flowing to rivals whose posture is exposure: Instinct raised US$350 million at US$2.5 billion two days after a report that it held users' email in plain text under a perpetual training licence verified [36] to-verify [37], and Hark raised US$700 million before a product to-verify [37]. The record's observation that no rival publishes a masking policy is correct; the market's revealed preference in 2026 is that it does not yet care.

The wedge remains defensible for the acts no platform will touch: identity acts, institutional filings, the counter, the parent's hospital, anything that spans several institutions or a border. Those are also the smallest and slowest to compound, which is exactly the ceiling DR-019 rejected when it declined to stay a specialist. The honest reframing is that the everyday endgame is a hypothesis with a kill criterion, and that the company must clear the return bar on the wedge alone.
