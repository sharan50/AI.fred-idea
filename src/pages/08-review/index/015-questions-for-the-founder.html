15Questions for the founder

These are the questions we would put in the first meeting, in the order we would ask them. Several test whether the founder owns the architecture the AI wrote; the rest test customer discovery, the numbers the record refuses, and the decisions this review says should be reversed.

  1. Walk us through the substitutor's ordered checks before a placement, and finding 3 of your own surfacing report, the placement that completes under a superseded envelope, without the document open: what is the gap, and how would you close it?
  2. Name the last five people you spoke to about this who are not Jash: a customer who lost a task to a lapsed policy, a bank or hospital that would let a data-centre browser fill its form, a vault vendor, a telephony vendor and a lawyer. What did each say that changed the record?
  3. How many wedge-class events did your own household and your ten closest prospective customers face in the last twelve months, and how many of those would they have delegated to a stranger for money? What does a dropped one cost, per event, in rupees or pounds and in hours, and what is the institutional source?
  4. Under your own pilot rules only India can exercise an identity act. Do you accept that India-first is forced, and what then are the UK and the US doing in the seed budget?
  5. Has Indian counsel confirmed that a private entity may state an identity number or complete a KYC form under a recorded mandate, given that video identification is the bank's own officials' act, and why does the design hold an Aadhaar number in a bought vault at all under the December 2025 regulations?
  6. Yohana closed on human hours at about US$100 a month. What is your guess, today, for minutes of L1 to L3 time on a KYC refresh and a hospital admission, and what price would a Bengaluru professional or an NRI child pay for each? DR-015 says you will not model it; say the number anyway and tell us what would make you wrong.
  7. What is the smallest floor that satisfies DR-006 and the two-person L3 zone on all three shifts, in heads and in rupees a year, and at what task volume does it reach 70 per cent utilisation? Given the record's own words that the room is never the closure, which site control would you drop first if the floor had to cost half as much?
  8. Who is the technical co-founder, which of the sixteen built components would they own from day one, and have they shipped a tokenisation or PCI boundary or a real-time media pipeline before?
  9. Have you prototyped the re-voiced, delayed transcript with real Indian booking-line audio? What was the masked-span rate and the added delay, and did a worker complete the call?
  10. Which twenty institutional websites in the pilot market have you driven with a browser agent from a data-centre address, and what fraction challenged or blocked the session? What is the plan when the managed browser is refused: human hands at human cost?
  11. Table 3.6 lists paths by which a person on your side could see a value. Where is the threat model for a compromised party page, a third-party script reading the field you just filled, a crafted reply in the user's inbox, a SIM-swap attacker re-binding a device, or a user who is a scam victim or a synthetic identity?
  12. In the United States, will the assistant ever say it is the customer to a bank, or answer security questions in the customer's name? If never, what fraction of the wedge survives once every call opens with an agency disclosure and the institution can refuse, and what instrument gives the agent its authority?
  13. Which FCA-authorised provider will you be the registered agent of for the UK, and have they said in writing that they would accept a Bengaluru-based processor within their due diligence and their professional indemnity cover?
  14. Walk us through the binding for a 72-year-old in Pune with worn fingerprints, no smartphone of her own and a registered mobile that is her son's: which screen does she see, who holds the phone, and what does the delegation record say about who tapped? When your worker then calls her bank and the desk asks who is calling, what exactly is said?
  15. The model vendor's policy names a qualified professional as the human in the loop for consumer-facing finance, legal and health outputs. Is an L3 worker one? What did the vendor say when you asked?
  16. What does a US$3 to 5 million seed buy, line by line: harness stages, pilot cohort, counsel per market, entities, insurance, reserve; how many months does that leave against a seed-to-A interval now over two years; and which three milestones will you show a Series A investor that can fail in a way you would accept as a reason to stop?
  17. The trust story says no person sees a number; the surfacing report lists two paths where one could. Which sentence will you change before you show the page to a customer, and what is your kill criterion for the DR-019 endgame if ninety-day repeat use stays below one task a month?
  18. What would make you abandon the harness-first order and ship one task type end to end on a bought vault, a bought browser and a ledger in ninety days?
