11Security and trust: a red-team read of the design

The trust design is unusually well thought through for a pre-product venture, and its central mechanism holds up: one detokenising component, a device-signed envelope, aliases everywhere else, and a ledger written from component records rather than from people. Read as an attacker, though, the record has a scoping flaw that shapes everything after it. Table 3.6 in 03 is, by its own title, the list of paths by which a human could see a real value, meaning a human on the operator's side. There is no adversary-centric threat model at all: no compromised party website, no third-party script on the bank's page reading the field the substitutor just typed into, no attacker who is the user (a scam victim tapping the envelope, or a synthetic identity using the harness to run KYC at scale), no lawful-access request to the vault vendor, no supply-chain compromise of the signed build. Twenty-six paths, seven adversarial passes and 123 findings were spent almost entirely on insider visibility, which is the one problem the architecture already solves by construction, while the problems the market has actually been losing on since 2025 (indirect prompt injection into browser agents, mail-borne zero-click injection, bribed offshore support staff, agents that fire irreversible acts against explicit instruction) are bounded in a single paragraph or absent.

The prompt-injection paragraph in 02, 3.4 is the record's weakest load-bearing claim. It is correct that a hostile page cannot mint an approval token, so an irreversible act cannot be fired by injection alone. But on a reversible step inside the envelope the ai executor can navigate within the party's domains, type, and request placement of an alias into any field whose declared kind or label matches, and labels are attacker-controlled DOM. The field the substitutor fills is readable by the page's own scripts, which the record requires so the site's validation runs, and third-party scripts on the party's page are explicitly loaded. Meta's Rule of Two says an agent that processes untrusted input, touches private data and changes state must be supervised verified [107]; the executor has all three and is supervised only when the step is irreversible. The best-funded browser agent still fell to 11.2 per cent of adaptive attacks after mitigation in August 2025 and blocks financial-services sites by default verified [6]; its maker's rival called the problem frontier and unsolved and later said it is unlikely ever to be fully solved. A serious reviewer will not let ai steps run on bank or government portals in a pilot without a per-action human check or an isolation design the record does not describe. Inbound mail is the second door: every reply fetched by the connector or forwarded to the relay reaches the planner through the edge proxy, screened by a detector for values, not for instructions; the planner never holds a real value and cannot mint a token, so the residual is a poisoned plan or a steered re-plan rather than exfiltration, and EchoLeak (CVE-2025-32711, a single crafted email executing hidden instructions once pulled into an assistant's context) is the precedent verified [108].

Three further matters change the plan rather than kill it. First, the legal basis for speaking to an institution as the customer is delegated to-verify in every market and is what the India pilot runs on; the record has not read a single bank's terms in any market, the RBI's 2017 circular puts the loss on a customer who shared credentials verified [45], and the illustrative health card in 04 already uses disclosed agency (I am calling on behalf of a patient), which should be the rule rather than the exception. Second, the tokenisation vendor is a single point of trust whose closures are contractual; a production order served on the vendor, a request under the CLOUD Act to a US-headquartered provider, or a provider insider is a detokenisation the substitutor never made and the ledger will not show, and the record's line that nobody sees your values is honestly nobody on our side. Third, the real crown jewel is not the substitutor but the alias-zone task service that writes step records and signs step contexts: an attacker inside that account can direct any alias in any step to any field on a contactable party's domain, because the envelope names act types and parties, not fields, and the step record is both the allow-list and the thing the task service writes; the penetration-test scope should centre there. Economics being deferred means there is no make-good policy, no insurance and no reserve for the wrong irreversible act, in a market where AB 316 removes the defence that the AI did it verified [77].

The floor and the stream leak in ways the canary test would not catch. Bribed offshore support staff are a proven, priced attack: two support agents at a vendor in India photographed screens for Coinbase customers in 2025, exposing tens of thousands of customers at an estimated cost of US$180 to 400 million, without a single password leaking verified [109] to-verify [110]; a worker on the Bengaluru floor holds enough non-sensitive task detail (institution, desk, timing, relationship words, a pseudonym per task) to be worth bribing even though no value is visible, and the record's own words are that the room is the record and the deterrent, never the closure. Run-sized masks on the browser stream encode string length and partial glyph width, which is an established attack class on redacted text verified [111]; the value-placed timer and the tone-for-span replacement on telephony give digit and span counts; and a fee-inside-sub-cap comparison beside a spend-cap card leaks a bound on an amount. The device binding models the user only as a legitimate principal: a new device binds and unbinds the old with no cooling-off period, no out-of-band notice, no velocity limit on identity acts after a re-bind, and no fraud-typology screening, so a SIM-swap attacker's afternoon with the product runs from re-bind to an envelope that adds a new bank. Finally the specification's own adversarial loop never converged: 29, 22, 13, 14, 16, 13 and 16 findings across seven passes, with sixteen open, two of them paths on which a person could see a value [REC].
