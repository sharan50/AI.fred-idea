9Business model and unit economics: the numbers the record defers

The record defers economics by decision (05, DR-015) and the deferral is internally coherent: every number would be a guess before a pilot, and the harness is built so that minutes per step and pool utilisation fall out of the step trace. A partner cannot accept it, because the design pages already fix the cost structure, and they fix it in the most expensive shape available. DR-006 and 04, 2 require one office running three shifts on a 24-hour wheel, each shift carrying an L1 pool, L2 pods for the markets held, at least one L3 seat inside a two-person zone, a shift lead and escalation cover. Rostered seven days a week with leave cover, that is a floor of roughly 24 heads in the leanest reading and about 43 in the reading the record describes, before the first customer [EST]. Built up from Bengaluru pay excerpts, statutory on-costs of 18 to 22 per cent, night-shift transport and a seat at the city's warm-shell rents, that is about Rs 2.9 to 6.8 crore a year (US$330,000 to 780,000) of fixed labour and site cost [EST] to-verify [50]. At a pilot of, say, 200 households doing four tasks a month with 45 human-minutes each, the floor is 11 to 19 per cent utilised and labour alone is US$40 to 75 per task [EST]. Nothing in the record acknowledges that the trust architecture has a minimum size, or that the pilot will run at a utilisation that makes its cost per task meaningless as a pricing input.

The field has already run this experiment at consumer price points and lost. Milo, at about US$40 a month, is reported closed with the human-in-the-loop model blamed to-verify [87]; Panasonic closed Yohana at US$249 to-verify [2]; Duckbill, the survivor, moved from an unlimited plan to a credit meter in which a task consumes about five of the eight credits US$99 buys to-verify [81]. AI.fred's labour is Indian rather than American, which is the one structural advantage it holds: an L1 productive hour at US$4 to 6 and an L3 hour at US$9 to 13 against a US copilot [EST], with outsourced Indian voice capacity priced at US$6 to 14 an hour fully loaded to-verify [94]. The sketch in this review says that at 2,000 households paying US$149 a month, 45 human-minutes a task and 70 per cent floor utilisation, gross margin lands near 55 to 60 per cent; at 90 minutes it falls below 30 per cent; at 15 minutes it exceeds 75 per cent [EST]. Minutes of human time per task is therefore the single variable the pilot must measure. The record is right that only a pilot can measure it; the record is wrong that the number can wait for a Series A, because the Series A will be raised on it.

Technology cost is not the problem. With Sonnet 5 at US$2 and US$10 per million tokens verified [18], browser sandboxes at about US$0.10 to 0.12 an hour to-verify [17] and carrier minutes at a cent or two, the model, sandbox and telephony cost of a multi-step task is a few dollars at most [EST]. The bought vault is a fixed cost that the per-region rule in DR-008 multiplies: Basis Theory's starter tier is US$995 a month for 20,000 active tokens and US-only, with regional hosting and PII handling on a custom-quoted enterprise tier verified [15], so three regions plausibly cost US$150,000 to 450,000 a year [EST]. The legal pack per market, compliance attestations and insurance are the other fixed lines the record leaves unpriced: DPDP duties bite from 13 May 2027 verified [38], SOC 2 or ISO 27001 is a company-level cost of US$20,000 to 50,000 in year one [EST], and standalone AI liability cover exists (a Lloyd's coverholder with limits reported to US$25 million) with no published premium and, for a pre-loss-history start-up executing identity acts, uncertain availability to-verify [95].

The customer-acquisition arithmetic is what a partner weighs hardest. Consumer fintech acquisition cost is reported at US$1,340 to 2,140 to-verify [96]; at US$149 a month and 57 per cent gross margin the contribution is about US$83 a month, payback about twenty months, and at 5 per cent monthly churn the lifetime value is roughly equal to the acquisition cost [EST]. Direct-to-consumer does not work on those numbers. The channels that do (an employer benefit at a per-member rate, a private bank or wealth manager paying per member as Ten Lifestyle's roughly £175 per active member a year shows to-verify [89]) pay a fraction of the consumer price and make the floor's utilisation the whole game. A seed of US$3 to 5 million (2026 medians) is consumed before the first paying task by the eight build stages, the floor, the vault and the first legal pack, at this review's estimate of US$2.5 to 4.5 million [EST], so the venture arrives at its Series A with pilot verdict data, a floor at low utilisation and a margin not yet demonstrated, which is precisely the moment DR-015 says the question reopens. The bar it will meet is US$2 to 4 million of annual recurring revenue verified [97], and at Faff's price that is 7,000 to 14,000 paying households, with graduation from seed to a priced next round at 16 to 24 per cent for recent cohorts and a median interval of 616 days to-verify [98] verified [97].

The deferral should be reversed for a range-based model now, with every number labelled an estimate and every design choice that drives cost given its price. There is also a supply-side why-now the record misses: Indian IT-services headcount tightened in 2025 and customer-service seats are being cut abroad, so the voice-process labour a floor of 24 to 43 needs is, for now, available to-verify [99]. That is not the full economics DR-015 reserves for a later round; it is the seed memo's minimum: a floor sized to the pilot, a vault region count, a legal-pack line, a make-good reserve, a minutes-per-task range with the price per event that clears it, and a channel hypothesis. The one arithmetic point to hold onto: at one to three wedge events a household a year and US$40 to 75 of labour per task at pilot utilisation, the avoided harm per event has to be worth well over US$100 to the customer for a per-event price to clear cost with margin, and no monthly subscription at any comparable's price funds it. The wedge is priced like an accountant's or a solicitor's fee, not like Faff or Duckbill; the demand case the partner needs is therefore the price of the dropped task per event, a frozen NRE account, a repudiated claim, a missed HMRC deadline, and that is the number the record lacks.
