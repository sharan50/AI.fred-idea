On this page

1Decision

For workflows we have modelled, the worker is given a structured task card derived from the page rather than a stream of its pixels: the fields the step needs, with their labels, their validity state and their aliases. The substitution component places the real values at submit, as it does on every surface. Masking then happens at the data layer, where it is deterministic, rather than at the pixel layer, where it is probabilistic.

Pixel streaming with a compositor-level mask and a delay line is deferred as the fallback for unmodelled sites, to be built only when volume justifies it. Until then an unmodelled counterparty is an ai step where the conditions of 02, 3.1 hold, a card the library is extended to cover, or a bounce. The specification survives in 02, 4.1 and 03, 3.1 as the fallback's specification, marked as deferred where it is stated.

2Rejected alternative(s) and their cost

A bespoke browser build with a mask and a delay line as a founding component
A forked Chromium, compositor hooks, a masker that paints by default and holds a frame it cannot clear, and read-back disabled on every channel but the driver's. Its cost was owning a browser and a new security surface, for masking that is probabilistic where the data layer is deterministic.
Letting a worker drive an unmasked page and trusting the floor
Rejected: it puts a value on a screen, which is the first fatal failure.
Modelling nothing and streaming everything
The published position. Its cost is the masker, its latency and its residual, on every counterparty, for ever.

3What this closes off

  • A counterparty the library has not modelled cannot be worked as a card, so coverage is an engineering backlog rather than a configuration, and the library is load-bearing.
  • A ticket must be raised when a counterparty changes its process, and the step is worked by hand or bounced until it lands. That cost is named in 02, 4.4 and 04, 3.1 and priced in 05.
  • The compositor mask, the delay line and the forked browser as founding components, and the media engineer as a founding hire.
  • Any claim that the pilot demonstrates pixel masking. It does not; the pilot demonstrates the card.

4Status

Fixed decision, taken by the founder on 2026-09-13. DR-021 is the same revision's answer on telephony; the substitution component, the envelope, the classifier and the state machine are unchanged by both.

AI.fred is a personal AI that builds your context, task by task, until it can act in your personal life the way someone who has known you for years would. It handles the ordinary things, a table, a return, a rental search, a trip, and it is built so that it can also be trusted with the ones you dread: the bank, the visa, the claim, the admission. The dreaded tasks are why people come. The ordinary ones are why they stay, and why the context compounds fast enough to be worth anything. AI.fred is a working title. This publication is a design record, not an offer.
