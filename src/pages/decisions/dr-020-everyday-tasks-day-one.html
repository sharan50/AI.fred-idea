On this page

1Decision

The product serves the user's everyday economic life and their consequential institutional tasks from launch, in one subscription, without staging. Restaurants and bookings, returns and refunds, travel, rentals, purchase research, appointments and outings are most of the volume; identity renewals, visas, bank and insurance admin, hospital coordination and tax notices are why a household signs up. Both run on one harness, one envelope and one classifier, which finds most everyday steps reversible and runs them as ai steps with no worker and no dual control.

The scope is stated in 00, 1 and the taxonomy in 01, 2; DR-019 carries the reasoning and DR-003's ceiling is unchanged by this record.

2Rejected alternative(s) and their cost

A wedge-first sequence, everyday tasks later
Rejected for the reasons in DR-019: the profile never compounds, no habit forms, and a subscription cannot be justified by occasional rescue.
Two products, or two brands
Two harnesses, two trust stories and a context split in two. The profile is worth something because it is one.
Everyday tasks as an unpriced extra on the consequential plan
Rejected because delivery cost scales with usage rather than with users, which is the whole finding of 05 and the reason DR-024 meters the allowance.

3What this closes off

  • A staged product plan, in any market, in which everyday tasks arrive after the consequential ones.
  • An unlimited promise on the everyday layer, which DR-024 prices as an allowance instead.
  • The claim that the competitive set is only the concierge field: from day one it includes services charging a fifth of our price and platform agents charging nothing.
  • Any argument that delivery volume per user is a second-order number. It rises by roughly an order of magnitude under this record, which makes cost per task the governing constraint on the company.

4Status

Fixed decision, taken by the founder on 2026-09-13. The volume it admits is what 05 models and DR-024 prices; DR-006 is staged in the same revision because the floor that serves the volume is a step cost.

AI.fred is a personal AI that builds your context, task by task, until it can act in your personal life the way someone who has known you for years would. It handles the ordinary things, a table, a return, a rental search, a trip, and it is built so that it can also be trusted with the ones you dread: the bank, the visa, the claim, the admission. The dreaded tasks are why people come. The ordinary ones are why they stay, and why the context compounds fast enough to be worth anything. AI.fred is a working title. This publication is a design record, not an offer.
