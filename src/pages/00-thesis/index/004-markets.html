4.1India as the build base

Three properties make India the right place to build and to run the floor. The engineering depth is there: Anthropic opened an office in Bengaluru in February 2026, naming India Claude's second-largest market verified [17]. A large English-speaking workforce means an operations floor whose day shift covers India and whose two night shifts cover the west, in one office under one set of site controls; the shift design in 04 draws the wheel. And India Stack is the richest set of consented rails for the acts our verticals need: DigiLocker for documents, the Account Aggregator framework for financial data and UPI Autopay for payment mandates, the brief's fixed list as 02 draws it; a delegated UPI debit itself is the user's own act on their own device today, on UPI Circle or Reserve Pay (Table 0.1). Those rails are gated: DigiLocker releases a document only after the user authorises the requester in their own session verified [18], and only an entity regulated by a financial-sector regulator may pull data through an Account Aggregator verified [19]. That shapes the adapter, which reaches the rails through the user's own device or a regulated partner and never around them, and anything a rail returns terminates at the vault's own proxy in front of the adapter, so neither the adapter nor the planner ever holds a raw byte. The gating does not shrink the ceiling.

4.2The US and the UK as commercial ground

Willingness to pay for delegation is established in both countries: a hybrid assistant sector has existed for a decade and premium concierge is a listed business (Table 0.2). The institutions are numerous, slow and paper-bound, and increasingly reached through a single identity; the UK is consolidating government access on GOV.UK One Login and brought a digital-verification trust framework into force on 1 September 2026 to-verify. Liability is firming in our favour: a business answers for its agent, and payment regulation is being rewritten with agents in mind (Table 0.1). The adapters differ from India's, Open Banking and identity rails in the UK, bank-data aggregation and card tokenisation in the US, both to-verify in the passports section of 03. In all three markets, under today's rails, any act that needs an OTP, a biometric, a PIN or a bank's own login bounces to the user's device by design; an OTP is never ours, and the only login we ever hold is one to a non-financial portal the user has expressly delegated, kept in the vault as an alias and placed by the system where the flag permits. Full delegation is the designed ceiling, fixed in DR-003, and the per-jurisdiction flag table in 03 decides at runtime which acts are delegated, which bounce and which are prohibited pending a regulatory answer; that table is also the lobbying agenda, line by line.

4.3One core, three adapters

The planner, the classifier, the task cards, the alias vault and substitution, the envelopes, the state machine, verification, the worker console, the ledger, the intake gateway and the policy layer's mechanism are one build and country-agnostic, as 02 lists them; the flag table the mechanism reads is the per-market half. Per country we add a passport adapter, a legal pack and a shift, and the expansion playbook in 06 repeats that triplet for every market after the first. Residency is designed for: Bengaluru staff handling UK and EU personal data is a restricted cross-border transfer, one more reason the vault, with its regional hosting, is bought; the data-residency map in 03 sets it out.
