7The scope, and what it gives up

What the previous edition called the endgame is the starting scope, not a later phase. AI.fred acts across the whole of a user's personal economic life from the first day and builds the profile of section 2 as it does so; the consequential tasks are the acquisition hook and the proof of trustworthiness, not the boundary of what the product does. DR-019 and DR-020 fix it, and this section records what the claim costs.

7.1The everyday tasks on the same harness

Below the line the two fatal failures draw sit the errands and bookings of section 1, and they run on the harness built for what sits above it: the same envelope, the same aliases, the same ledger, the same classifier. What the classifier finds is different: most everyday steps are reversible, so they run as ai steps with no worker and no dual control, and the point-of-no-return test answers yes at once. The argument of section 6 against the generalist stands, because it was never about how many kinds of task an assistant takes; it was about an assistant that sees everything. Ours takes more and sees nothing. The previous edition gated the everyday tasks on each market's legal pack opening them, and that gate was the wrong way round: a pack governs identity and institutional acts (06), which are the consequential tasks, not the everyday ones.

7.2The asset

Every task teaches the context store a little more, with source, date and confidence (DR-016), and the store is what lets the next task, and the everyday ones, run with fewer questions. That accumulation is why the first port of call is a result and not a positioning: the assistant that already holds a person's affairs, with provenance the person can audit, is the one they turn to first. The store is held for the user, under the purpose 03 records, and the eight properties of section 6.3 are unchanged; the store compounds behind them.

7.3What the data may and may not be used for

The previous edition left open a second shape of company, monetising the accumulated picture of people's affairs once the base was large enough. DR-019 now settles it, and two of the three uses that sat inside that idea survive. Data used to improve the product is the highest-value use by a wide margin and starts early, because it feeds the automation share the whole business rests on. Data used as commerce is real: a take rate on transactions the user already asked us to execute, bound by the one rule DR-024 makes non-negotiable, that a merchant may never pay for placement in a recommendation. Data sold as an asset is withdrawn entirely: it earns little beside the subscription, it is priced against a churn exposure several times larger in a product whose pricing power comes from being trusted with passports and bank statements, and purpose limitation under the DPDP Act and the GDPR does not stretch from executing a task to producing market intelligence. 05 carries the arithmetic and the scale at which each use arrives.
