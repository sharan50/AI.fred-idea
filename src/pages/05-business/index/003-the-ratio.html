3The one ratio that decides everything

Delivery cost scales with usage, not with users. The governing number is therefore human minutes per user per month, which is the product of promised task volume and blended handling time, set against average revenue per user.

At 158 minutes a month against a $37 average there is no scale at which the arithmetic recovers, because a generalist minute fully loaded costs roughly Rs 7 to Rs 16 depending on seniority mix and year, and the subscription buys only a few of them. At 61 minutes against a $70 average the business is a normal, capital-light services-plus-software company. assumption

Four parameters move the ratio, and they are the four the plan commits to. The run that shows what they are worth moves six others as well, which is set out in section 5, because the gap between the two configurations should not be read as though all of it were planned.
